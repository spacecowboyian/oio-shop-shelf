# Power Train — 1963 Passenger Cars

This chapter covers removal, separation, assembly, and installation of the rear engine/transaxle unit in a **1963 Chevrolet Corvair passenger car**. The 1962–63 supplement changes the base removal sequence and applies to the 1963 car. Three-speed and four-speed manual transmissions, Powerglide, turbocharged engines, and factory heating options are distinguished below. Corvair 95 commercial procedures are excluded. For internal repairs use [engine](06a-engine.md), [clutch](06b-clutch.md), [rear axle](06c-rear-axle.md), [manual transmission](06d-manual-transmission.md), or [Powerglide](06e-automatic-transmission.md). Original files, hashes, and page mapping are in the [source inventory](../data/source-inventory.json).

## Arrangement and 1963 changes

<a id="p232"></a> **[PDF p.232](../page-images/p0232.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-1 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The rear engine, differential carrier, and transmission form one power train supported at three mounting points. With a manual transmission the clutch shaft runs through the differential pinion shaft. Powerglide uses a torque converter and front-pump shaft. The sectional factory view is split across the next two pages. The base page's 1961 choke and axle-ratio descriptions are not 1963 specifications; use the [1963 specifications](12-specifications.md) and [engine chapter](06a-engine.md).

<a id="p658"></a> **[PDF p.658](../page-images/p0658.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=6-opening years=1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The supplement makes these changes to removal of the complete power train:

- Omit base removal step 6, the manual choke disconnection: the 1963 car has an automatic choke.
- At step 7a, disconnect the **battery positive cable** and the separate wire from battery positive to the voltage regulator **BATT** terminal **at the regulator**.
- At step 8, raise the vehicle on a hoist; **do not disconnect the starter-solenoid wires** for complete-unit removal. The exhaust and muffler removal in base step 22 remains required.
- On a turbocharged passenger car, remove the supercharger–carburetor assembly and air cleaner to clear the front seal and heater duct. Disconnect the manifold-pressure tube at the manifold and feed it through the front shield while lowering or raising the unit. Disconnect the usual electrical connectors, battery, and coil. The same jack adapter supports the engine.

## Remove the complete power train

<a id="p235"></a> **[PDF p.235](../page-images/p0235.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-4 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

**Support warning:** The assembly weighs approximately **460 lb**. Use engine support fixture **J-7894** on a suitable hydraulic jack, such as J-8394. Support the engine **at its pan rail, never at the oil pan**. The balance point is approximately **0.200 in. behind the cylinder block's front face**. Lock the fixture to the jack and lower gradually, watching for interference.

1. Remove the spare tire from the passenger-car engine compartment. Remove both air cleaners and, where fitted, the air-heater return hose and engine-to-body front seal retainer.
2. Disconnect the carburetor return spring and throttle linkage. Leave the automatic choke intact; the manual-choke step in the base page was deleted by the supplement.
3. Disconnect the battery positive cable and the positive-to-regulator BATT wire **at the regulator**, as directed by the 1962–63 supplement. Disconnect the other engine electrical connectors called out by Figure 6-3, including the coil/engine harness and grounds, marking them for installation.

<a id="p236"></a> **[PDF p.236](../page-images/p0236.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-5 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

4. Raise the vehicle. Keep starter-solenoid wiring attached during complete-unit removal, per the supplement. Remove the rear grille, skid plate and engine rear/side seal retainers as needed for clearance.
5. Unhook the parking-brake return spring at the front engine mount and separate the accelerator-control rods. Disconnect and plug the engine fuel line, and disconnect the speedometer cable at the differential carrier. Disconnect flexible air-heater hoses where fitted. Omit the base manual-choke-cable step.

[Figure 6-3](../diagrams/p0236-engine-electrical-connections.png) identifies the engine electrical connections. Wire colors and terminals should be checked against the car's actual harness and [electrical section](08-electrical-systems.md).

<a id="p237"></a> **[PDF p.237](../page-images/p0237.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-6 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

6. On a gasoline-heater car, disconnect its fuel line. Release the universal joints and remove the axle shafts as described in [Section 6C](06c-rear-axle.md).
7. **Manual:** Disconnect the shift coupling, clutch return spring, fork pull rod, cross shaft, and cable. On an equipped four-speed, disconnect the backup-light switch, catching any escaping lubricant.
8. **Powerglide:** Disconnect the shift cable; rotate the throttle-valve (TV) lever fully counterclockwise to withdraw its cable end. Remove the filler tube and catch escaping fluid.
9. Position and lock J-7894 under the engine pan rail. The [factory connection and support figures](../diagrams/p0237-power-train-removal-connections.png) show the clutch linkage, Powerglide cable, and fixture.

<a id="p238"></a> **[PDF p.238](../page-images/p0238.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-7 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

10. Remove the mount cotter pins and castellated nuts. **Record and retain the exact front-mount shim stack:** changing it changes rear toe. Lower the assembly slowly, checking rear mount and left rear lower-arm clearance. Remove exhaust and muffler as the base procedure specifies; the supplement explicitly retains this step. Refer to [Section 9](09-fuel-and-exhaust.md).

For **manual transaxle separation**, support the transaxle with a sling. Drain transmission and differential; remove the starter as in [Section 8](08-electrical-systems.md). Disconnect clutch pull rod, seal, and pin. Unbolt the differential carrier from clutch housing and pull the transaxle **straight away horizontally**. Remove the clutch shaft, fork, and release bearing. To separate transmission from carrier, remove four bolts: two driven from transmission side and two from carrier side. Continue repairs in Sections [6C](06c-rear-axle.md) and [6D](06d-manual-transmission.md).

For **Powerglide separation**, disconnect the vacuum-modulator tube/hose and remove the starter. Through the converter-housing access hole, turn the converter by its starter teeth to expose the three converter-to-flex-plate bolts; remove them.

<a id="p239"></a> **[PDF p.239](../page-images/p0239.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-8 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Support the automatic transaxle by a sling, unbolt the carrier from converter housing, and draw it straight away. **Catch and secure the converter immediately** so it cannot fall. Remove the turbine shaft. To separate Powerglide from the differential carrier, pull the turbine shaft without damage, remove the governor and remaining three bolts, and separate straight back to protect the pump shaft and bushings. Retain gasket, governor gear and selective spacers in order. For internal work, see Sections [6C](06c-rear-axle.md) and [6E](06e-automatic-transmission.md).

## Assemble the transaxle and engine

<a id="p240"></a> **[PDF p.240](../page-images/p0240.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-9 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

**Powerglide end play:** Select thrust washers at **one location only**. When differential and transmission were separated, select at the **rear governor-gear location** with J-8364. When they were not separated, select at the **front clutch-drum/front-pump location** with J-8371 and the detailed Section 6E procedure. Never gauge or compensate at both locations for the same repair. The rear selection procedure follows below.

**Manual transmission and differential:** Apply a new gasket with petroleum jelly. Engage transmission mainshaft splines with the pinion's internal splines. Fit all four bolts, two from each side, and tighten to **24–32 ft-lb**. Fit release bearing and fork, then clutch shaft. With the shaft fully engaged, the distance between the end of the clutch-release-bearing shaft and the clutch-shaft end is **2 9/16 ± 1/16 in.** Support the transaxle with a sling, align the differential carrier to the clutch housing, and draw it straight into place.

<a id="p241"></a> **[PDF p.241](../page-images/p0241.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-10 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Finish manual assembly by engaging clutch-disc/pilot splines, connecting pull rod and dust seal, installing starter, and refilling transmission and differential. Adjust clutch linkage by the detailed [Section 6B procedure](06b-clutch.md#clutch-arrangement-and-adjustment). **Source conflict:** This chapter's vehicle-installation text says to back the fork-rod swivel off **two turns**; Section 6B's detailed adjustment says **three turns**. This manual uses Section 6B for the final adjustment; verify the factory pages before relying on the differing count.
<!-- NEEDS REVIEW: Factory Section 6 installation says two fork-rod swivel turns; detailed Section 6B adjustment says three. Confirm for 1963 before relying on the count. -->

**Powerglide rear selective thrust washer, when carrier and transmission have been separated:** Target running clearance is **0.025–0.045 in.** Adjust the low band before standing the transmission on its front end. Use J-8364 and a dial indicator with 3-in. extension, **without a gasket**. Zero on the planet-carrier hub; lifting the tool off must deflect the indicator no more than **0.050 in.** Move the fixture to the governor gear and read the indicated difference. The factory [gauge setup and selection chart](../diagrams/p0241-powerglide-thrust-selection.png) govern washer choice; select the listed count of **0.016 ± 0.001 in.** spacers. If the first reading is below **0.025 in.**, replace the front clutch-hub/front-pump thrust washer with the **0.050 in.** washer, then repeat the **rear** selection from the start. Recheck after installing spacers for **0.025–0.045 in.** clearance. Do not infer spacer count from the running-clearance target alone.

|---|---:|---:|
| 0.025–0.046 | None | None |
| 0.047–0.062 | 1 | 0.016 ± 0.001 |
| 0.063–0.078 | 2 | 0.032 ± 0.002 |
| 0.079–0.094 | 3 | 0.048 ± 0.003 |
| 0.095–0.110 | 4 | 0.064 ± 0.004 |
| 0.111–0.126 | 5 | 0.080 ± 0.005 |
| 0.127–0.142 | 6 | 0.096 ± 0.006 |
| 0.143–0.155 | 7 | 0.112 ± 0.007 |

<a id="p242"></a> **[PDF p.242](../page-images/p0242.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-11 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

For Powerglide-to-carrier assembly, fit a new gasket with petroleum jelly. Guide the pump shaft in without scoring bushings; engage pinion/planet splines. Refit governor and four attaching bolts, starting the two carrier-side bolts first; torque bolts to **24–32 ft-lb**. Install turbine shaft through both spline sets and seat converter fully through its spline engagements. **Retain the converter with clips whenever the rear of the assembly is tipped downward.** Sling the unit, remove clips immediately before mating, pilot converter hub into crankshaft, and align the carrier/housing. Start the top-left housing bolt near the **11-o'clock** position. Fit the first converter-to-flex-plate bolt near **1 o'clock** to hold hole alignment.

<a id="p243"></a> **[PDF p.243](../page-images/p0243.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-12 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Finish Powerglide engine attachment with remaining housing bolts, starter, two remaining converter bolts via the access hole, and vacuum-modulator hose. Before raising the complete assembly, keep harness wires, fuel lines, and levers clear. Raise on J-7894 and seat mounts; tighten **front mounts to 60–80 ft-lb** and **rear mount to 50–60 ft-lb**, then fit cotter pins. Restore the **same front shim stack** to preserve rear toe; check [rear alignment](03-suspension.md) if disturbed.

For **Powerglide**, install filler tube and fill per [Section 6E](06e-automatic-transmission.md). Lubricate a **new O-ring** for the shift cable, rotate TV lever fully counterclockwise, seat the cable ball and shoulder, fasten cable and grommet; when properly installed the TV-lever hole is about **3/8 in. below the pan rail**. For **manual**, reconnect shift coupling and set the shifter in first (three-speed) or fourth (four-speed) while tightening its clamp. Refit clutch cross shaft, cable, return spring, and pull rod. Use the detailed [6B adjustment](06b-clutch.md#clutch-arrangement-and-adjustment) for final fork-rod setting because of the two-turn/three-turn conflict noted above. Refit four-speed backup switch where equipped and fill transmission/differential to the filler holes with **SAE 80 multipurpose gear lubricant** as specified on this base page; check the [1963 lubrication chapter](02-lubrication.md) for service applicability.

<a id="p244"></a> **[PDF p.244](../page-images/p0244.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6-13 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Install axle shafts per 6C. Reconnect gasoline-heater fuel line and air-heater hoses **only when equipped**. Reconnect engine fuel and speedometer cables, accelerator rods, parking-brake spring, rear/side engine seals, skid plate, and grille. Reconnect coil, engine harness, grounds, and regulator BATT wire; attach the battery positive cable. Starter-solenoid wiring remains attached during complete-unit removal/installation under the supplement's revised sequence. Refit engine front seal, air-heater return hose (if equipped), air cleaners and spare tire. Start the engine and check for fluid/fuel leaks, secure wiring/hoses, mount seating, shift and clutch/Powerglide operation, and heater function as equipped. Omit the obsolete base manual-choke reconnection. The commercial-only procedure beginning at the foot of this factory page does not apply.

## Source issues to check

- The base Section 6 installation says **two turns** for the clutch fork-rod swivel; detailed Section 6B says **three turns**. Follow the detailed adjustment and confirm against the original pages for the car under service.
- The supplement alters battery/regulator disconnection and says starter-solenoid wiring need not be removed. The reverse sequence here reconnects only wiring actually disconnected.
- Section 6 base text includes early manual-choke steps and commercial instructions. They are inapplicable to this 1963 passenger-car chapter.
