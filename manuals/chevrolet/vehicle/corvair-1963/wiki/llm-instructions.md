# 1963 Chevrolet Corvair Shop Manual — reader instructions

This conversion is **in progress**. The [chapter index](00-index.md) marks
pending and partial coverage. Do not answer from an absent section or from raw
OCR as though it were a verified 1963 service procedure.

Use [manual-index.jsonl](../data/manual-index.jsonl) for available verified
facts. Each row must carry `_source_id`, `_original_page`, `_applicable_years`,
and `_scope`; require `1963` in `_applicable_years` and passenger scope. Cite the
cleaned chapter and the source document's printed page. If a row is absent,
explain the coverage gap rather than borrowing a 1961 or 1962 value.

The [source inventory](../data/source-inventory.json) and
[page map](../data/source-page-map.jsonl) identify every staged factory PDF and
the combined working PDF page. The 1962–63 supplement takes precedence where
it explicitly updates the base procedure. A 1963 column wins over a 1962
column. Commercial-only Corvair 95 procedures are excluded.

Review [needs-review](10-needs-review.md) before using a value that appears
ambiguous in the printed source.
