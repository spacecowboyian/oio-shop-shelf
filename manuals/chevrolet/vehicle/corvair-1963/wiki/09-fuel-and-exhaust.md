# Fuel and Exhaust Systems — 1963 Passenger Cars

The 1963 passenger reference uses the supplement's **automatic-choke
Rochester H** carburetor for regular engines and the **Carter YH plus
turbo-supercharger** for the Spyder option. The 1961 manual-choke
carburetor instructions are superseded. The supplement explicitly
retains the base procedures for fuel tank, lines, pump, exhaust and
muffler where it does not give an update. The 1963 model list cancels
station wagons; Corvair 95 commercial material is outside this
reference. The original PDFs and their page mappings are in the
[source inventory](../data/source-inventory.json).

## Inherited mechanical fuel pump

<a id="p517"></a> **[PDF p.517](../page-images/p0517.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-17 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The engine-mounted AC diaphragm pump is driven by a push rod bearing
on a crankshaft eccentric; its return spring is in the push-rod
assembly. Periodically check pump mounting, cover bolts and fuel
connections. Diagnose it on the engine with fuel in the tank. The
tank-to-pump line is under **suction**, so a leak there may reduce
delivery without visible drips; pump-to-carburetor lines are under
pressure. This basic pump service carries forward under the
supplement's explicit Section 9 instruction.

<a id="p518"></a> **[PDF p.518](../page-images/p0518.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-18 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Inspect loose fittings, kinks and diaphragm-flange screws first.
Disable ignition and crank with carburetor feed lines directed into
suitable containers to establish whether fuel is delivered; then
separate blocked pipes from pump failure. The base flow criterion
is superseded by the 1963 tune-up
specification of **one pint in 30–45 seconds**
([supplement p.685](../page-images/p0685.png)). Pressure remains
**4–5 psi** at roughly **500–1,000 rpm**, constant through that
range. Remove the pump if pressure is low, high or materially varies.
For removal on the **two-carburetor regular engine**, disconnect inlet
and the two outlet lines at the T. On a Spyder, disconnect its installed
single-carburetor feed arrangement. Loosen the locknut, withdraw set
screw, and lift the pump and push rod carefully from the housing.
Plug its openings before exterior cleaning.

| Inherited pump pressure test | Factory value |
|---|---|
| Pressure, idle to 1,000 rpm | 4–5 psi |

<a id="p519"></a> **[PDF p.519](../page-images/p0519.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-19 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Disassemble the diaphragm cover, spring and diaphragm/body unit;
separate the lower body and pulsator diaphragm. Clean metal parts and
passages, inspect cracks, distortion and threads, and replace damaged
parts. Reassemble valves properly staked, pulsator diaphragm, body,
spring and cover; the offset bolt hole indexes the pieces. Fit the
pump with its outlet forward (the regular engine uses a T connector),
engage set screw in its locking hole,
tighten the set screw to **9–15 ft-lb** and its locknut to the same
range, then reconnect lines.

<a id="p520"></a> **[PDF p.520](../page-images/p0520.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-20 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Fuel leakage points to loose screws or fittings, damaged threads or a
torn fuel diaphragm; oil leakage points to diaphragm or oil-seal
damage. Poor delivery also calls for line and fitting checks. Pump
noise can result from loose mounting.

## Air cleaner

<a id="p521"></a> **[PDF p.521](../page-images/p0521.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-21 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

The standard 1963 passenger air cleaner uses an **oil-wetted
polyurethane element**. Remove the wheel/tire for access, release wing
clamps and covers, lift out the element, inspect for tears, and clean
the housing. Wash the element in kerosene or mineral spirits, **squeeze**
out solvent, dip in engine oil and squeeze out excess oil; avoid hot
degreaser and acetone-type solvents. Refit the element without folds
or leaks at its lips. The base service interval is superseded by
the [1963 lubrication schedule](02-lubrication.md#p637-cooling), which
sets **6,000 miles** or sooner in dusty conditions. That schedule
explicitly includes the turbocharged engine.

<a id="p522"></a> **[PDF p.522](../page-images/p0522.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-22 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine applicability=air-cleaner-continuation -->

Never shake, swing or wring the polyurethane element; **squeeze** it.
Seat both lower and upper lips when refitting element and cover.
The lower half of this page describes Corvair 95/Greenbrier and
models **535/735 station wagon** intake arrangements. Commercial
vehicles are outside scope, and the 1963 factory model list cancels
the passenger station wagons; do not use that alternate installation
for a 1963 passenger coupe, sedan or convertible.

## Passenger fuel tank and lines

<a id="p523"></a> **[PDF p.523](../page-images/p0523.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-23 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The passenger tank sits under the front compartment floor behind the
front crossmember, held by a single strap, and fills at the left
front fender. Pickup and sender are one unit with a fine-mesh inlet
strainer. Capacity is **14 gallons**, confirmed in the 1962–63
supplement [Section 12 p.635](../page-images/p0635.png). There is no
drain plug: remove the sender to drain. When refitting toe-pan cover
screws, keep them from puncturing the tank.

| 1963 passenger fuel tank | Factory value |
|---|---|
| Capacity | 14 gallons |

<a id="p524"></a> **[PDF p.524](../page-images/p0524.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-24 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The line runs from the tank sender through the covered tunnel to the
rear crossmember and then to the engine fuel pump. Inspect for leaks,
kinks and dents. For removal, take off the tunnel cover, disconnect
front and rear hose joints, plug pickup to limit draining, release
the rear clips and tunnel clamps, and withdraw the line. Blow out a
line if carburetor or pump inspection reveals dirt; refit grommets and
clamps when installing.

<a id="p525"></a> **[PDF p.525](../page-images/p0525.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-25 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger applicability=upper-page -->

Replace tunnel clamps removed during line service and reconnect both
ends before closing the cover. To service the sender/strainer,
disconnect signal and ground wires, drain tank by releasing the
sender cam with J-8950, and remove the unit. Blow debris from the
strainer or replace it if damaged. Check the sender terminal's
spanner nut for tightness to prevent leakage. To remove the tank,
drain it, free the filler-neck seal and one strap nut, lift the left
side, and work the tank down right side first. Refit with the
specified **1-inch gap** between filler pipe and tank neck inside
the connecting hose. The lower page begins commercial-only tank
service and is excluded.

<a id="p526"></a> **[PDF p.526](../page-images/p0526.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-26 scope=commercial; excluded=passenger -->

The Corvair 95/Greenbrier tank, filler position and **18.6-gallon**
capacity do not apply to the 1963 passenger car.

## Passenger exhaust

<a id="p527"></a> **[PDF p.527](../page-images/p0527.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-27 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger applicability=exhaust-only -->

The upper half continues commercial tank service and is excluded.
The lower half describes the regular passenger single exhaust:
packing-flange joints at both manifolds join to a single right-side
muffler and tailpipe. For exhaust assembly removal, free the four
manifold mounting nuts and the engine-bracket bolt, then draw the
assembly from below. The **turbocharged Spyder has its own exhaust
system** on supplement pp.741–742 and must use that procedure.

<a id="p528"></a> **[PDF p.528](../page-images/p0528.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=9-28 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger applicability=regular-exhaust-only -->

For a regular engine, install fresh packing at both manifold joints,
then tighten the four manifold nuts to **15–20 ft-lb** and engine
bracket bolt to **8–10 ft-lb**. A service muffler joins to the cut
original exhaust pipe by a clamp flange. The remainder of this page
describes commercial exhaust or old manual-choke tools and is
excluded.

## Regular-engine automatic-choke Rochester H carburetors

<a id="p713"></a> **[PDF p.713](../page-images/p0713.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-1 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

The 1962–63 regular engine has **two Rochester Model H single-barrel
downdraft automatic-choke carburetors**, one on each head's intake
manifold with an insulating block. Each automatic choke uses a
cylinder-head-mounted thermostatic coil and an air-horn vacuum
diaphragm. The source expressly says the tank, lines, fuel pump,
exhaust and muffler retain 1961 service. This carburetor procedure
does **not** apply to the Monza Spyder's Carter YH.

<a id="p714"></a> **[PDF p.714](../page-images/p0714.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-2 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

Synchronize the pair with the [1963 tune-up procedure](07-engine-tune-up.md#p682).
After curb idle is set, place the throttle lever on the **second
highest fast-idle cam step** and bend its tang for **.078 in.**
between idle screw and throttle lever. Hold the choke vacuum-diaphragm
arm against the diaphragm: clearance from choke-valve lower edge to
air-horn wall should be **.160–.175 in.**; adjust its link. At that
setting the fast-idle tang belongs on the second-highest step.
With throttle fully open, set choke unloader gap at **.250 in.**
by bending the throttle-lever tang.

<a id="p715"></a> **[PDF p.715](../page-images/p0715.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-3 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

With curb and fast-idle screws backed off and throttle fully closed,
the scribe line on the accelerator-pump lever must align to the cast
tang at the front of the bowl cover (Figure 9-9). Bend the pump rod
carefully to achieve that alignment and resynchronize carburetors.
Recheck the **.250-in. unloader gap** if the outer choke-shaft tang
was adjusted.

<a id="p716"></a> **[PDF p.716](../page-images/p0716.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-4 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

For float setting, remove bowl cover and gasket. Invert cover on a
flat surface and use **J-9577** over each float. Bend needle tang until
each float touches gauge, align them within **1/32 in.** parallel to
cover and center them between gauge legs. Without the gauge, the
factory prints level as **1 8/64 in.** from float top to gasket.
Upright, measure gasket to float bottom and bend hinge-end tang for
**1¾ in. float drop**. Recheck level, assemble cover, then synchronize
the pair. See the [factory float figures](../diagrams/p0716-rochester-h-float-setting.png).

| Regular-engine float setting | Factory value |
|---|---|
| Level, float top to gasket | 1 8/64 in. |
| Drop, gasket to float bottom | 1¾ in. |
| Float parallelism to cover | Within 1/32 in. |

<a id="p717"></a> **[PDF p.717](../page-images/p0717.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-5 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

The same procedure serves both carburetors. For removal, release air
cleaners, choke rods, accelerator return spring, accelerator rod and
cross-shaft rods; remove the cross-shaft support and fuel inlets,
disconnect right-carburetor vacuum-advance hose, then free two
carburetor nuts and lift from the manifold studs. Figures 9-14 and
9-15 identify the revised automatic-choke bowl and cover components.

<a id="p718"></a> **[PDF p.718](../page-images/p0718.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-6 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

For disassembly, detach pump rod, inlet nut/filter/spring and choke
levers; remove bowl cover and gasket, release vacuum diaphragm link,
then take out float pin, floats and needle. Remove pump, venturi
cluster, discharge valve, idle needle, main jet, choke and throttle
shafts only as needed. Preserve each part's location for assembly;
the exploded views identify the needle seat, diaphragm, accelerator
pump and choke link.

<a id="p719"></a> **[PDF p.719](../page-images/p0719.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-7 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

Clean metal castings in solvent and dry passages with compressed air.
**Do not immerse pump plunger or gaskets in carburetor cleaner; do not
ream fuel passages or air bleeds with wire or drills.** Replace worn
needle/seat, damaged floats, loose throttle shaft, scored pump well,
damaged choke parts or brittle gaskets. Ensure main-well nozzle and
idle tube are square to the body. Clean the inlet filter or replace
it if deformed or still plugged. Begin assembly with seated throttle
valve, venturi cluster/gasket and pump/discharge needle.

<a id="p720"></a> **[PDF p.720](../page-images/p0720.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-8 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

Refit choke valve and freely moving shaft, needle seat and needle,
floats and new gasket; set level/drop with J-9577. Install the bowl
cover with its six screws in original positions, inlet filter and
pump rod. Recheck pump rod, vacuum break, unloader and fast-idle
clearance. Reinstall on insulator blocks and tighten the two mounting
nuts evenly. **Right** carburetor vacuum port connects to distributor;
cap the **left** port. Restore cross-shaft, fuel, accelerator and
choke rods; synchronize and service the air cleaners.

<a id="p721"></a> **[PDF p.721](../page-images/p0721.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-9 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=regular-engine -->

To replace a choke thermostat, remove lower engine shroud, free
bracket drive screw and slide thermostat from lower rod. Install new
thermostat on the rod, attach bracket and shroud, then set choke rod.
For poor carburetion, the factory chart begins with heat insulator,
mounting nuts, compression and ignition before float, pump-rod, choke,
jet and venturi checks. Its diagnosis includes vacuum-hose leakage,
open external vent, sticking choke, worn pump leather and dirty
filter; trace the specific symptom against the original chart.

<a id="p722"></a> **[PDF p.722](../page-images/p0722.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-10 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The supplement illustrates **J-9577** for Rochester H float setting,
**J-21056** for the Spyder Carter metering/float work, and
**J-21004** as a supercharger support.

## Monza Spyder: Carter YH and turbo-supercharger

The following pages apply **only to the turbocharged Spyder option**.
They do not change the regular-engine Rochester H procedure above.

<a id="p723"></a> **[PDF p.723](../page-images/p0723.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-11 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

The exhaust-driven turbo-supercharger routes exhaust through a turbine
that shares a shaft with the centrifugal impeller. The impeller
pressurizes the Carter YH air-fuel mixture entering the intake. Its
rotating group is precision balanced; inspect and handle it as a
matched assembly. The [original schematic](../diagrams/p0723-spyder-turbo-flow.png) shows air-fuel, exhaust
and oil flow.

<a id="p724"></a> **[PDF p.724](../page-images/p0724.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-12 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

The semi-floating sleeve bearing takes engine oil from the filter
adapter and drains into the right cylinder-head rocker area. For the
Spyder, use **premium fuel**. The [1963 lubrication schedule](02-lubrication.md#p637)
supersedes this page's earlier break-in oil and air-cleaner intervals.
Follow its temperature-dependent initial oil-drain instruction and
clean and reoil the Spyder polyurethane element on that schedule,
sooner in dusty service. Use kerosene or mineral spirits, squeeze
rather than wring, and renew a torn element. Remove the cleaner by
disconnecting its bracket, carburetor clamp and choke clean-air tube;
fit a **new O-ring** at the carburetor air horn on installation.
The separate inline Spyder fuel filter on the cleaner bracket is
replaced under the [1963 schedule](02-lubrication.md#p638),
which supersedes this page's earlier interval. Clean the Carter YH
inlet screen at the same time. Install the filter with its arrows in
the flow direction.

<a id="p725"></a> **[PDF p.725](../page-images/p0725.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-13 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

For throttle linkage, warm engine or hold choke open, detach the
accelerator swivel, seat throttle lever at idle screw, and set the
cross-shaft linkage angle to approximately **126°** with its
adjustable rod. Hold pedal linkage at its rear stop and carburetor at
full throttle, then set swivel to enter lever hole without preload.
Verify full-throttle travel and free return to idle. For idle/mixture,
warm engine, remove fast idle, attach tachometer and manifold vacuum
gauge, and alternate speed and mixture adjustments for the highest
steady vacuum at **850 rpm**. Restore distributor hose. If fitted,
the throttle-return check valve should leave **.030 in.** between
lever and its bolt at warm idle after final mixture setting; adjust
the self-locking bolt while holding the diaphragm stem.

<a id="p726"></a> **[PDF p.726](../page-images/p0726.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-14 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

For Carter YH float level, remove its six bowl-cover screws; with
cover inverted measure gasket surface to float center. The printed
setting is **⅝ in.**, or use J-21056. With float hanging, measure
gasket surface to float bottom at its free end and adjust the hinge
tang for **2⅜ in. ± 1⁄16 in.**; use a new bowl-cover gasket.
At operating temperature and **850 rpm**, set ignition timing to
**24° advance maximum**. The source says not to disconnect and plug
the distributor pressure-retard hose for this check. The page's
on-vehicle automatic-choke instruction reads **one notch lean** for the
1963 Spyder. The [1964 factory supplement, Section 9 p.9-14](https://northern-california-corvairs.com/64%20Shop%20Manual%20Ch%209-fuelExh-64.pdf)
repeats **one notch lean**, and the [Carter YH specification chart](https://www.carburetor-parts.com/assets/manuals/Carter-YH-Manual.pdf#page=24)
separates 1963–66 (**one notch lean**) from 1962 (**one notch rich**).
The assembly paragraph on p.733 still prints **one notch rich**; it is
retained below as an inconsistent instruction, not the 1963 final setting.

|---|---|

<a id="p727"></a> **[PDF p.727](../page-images/p0727.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-15 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

At routine service, inspect intake hoses, clamps and gaskets for
leaks/restrictions; exhaust connections for cracks or leaks; oil
feed/drain for damage; and the charger for smoke, unusual noise or
vibration. The factory calls for a major inspection at **50,000
miles**, or sooner for suspected trouble. Before removal, divert
the oil-drain elbow into a container and check flow at idle:
approximately **one quart per minute**. With assembly removed,
inspect turbine blades, shield and impeller for cracks, erosion,
rub marks, carbon and oil. Clean impeller/housing with a nylon
bristle brush and diesel fuel or kerosene; residual dirt can
unbalance the rotating group.

<a id="p728"></a> **[PDF p.728](../page-images/p0728.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-16 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Measure axial turbine-shaft end play with a dial indicator at
the impeller nut while pressing on the housing; repeat the reading.
Allowable end play is **.005–.008 in.** The factory diagnostic chart
separates low boost, smoke, noise, vibration, oil leakage and failure
to return to idle. Check air-cleaner restriction, intake/exhaust
leaks, rotating-group condition, oil seals, accelerator linkage and
timing before selecting a repair.

<a id="p729"></a> **[PDF p.729](../page-images/p0729.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-17 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Check radial shaft play at the impeller nut in two directions 90°
apart: **.022 in. maximum**; excess play or axial excess requires
rebuild. If serviceable, refit compressor housing with new gasket
and tighten six bolts to **80 in-lb**. To remove the Carter YH
separately, remove air cleaner; detach throttle-return valve vacuum,
choke heat, fuel and accelerator linkage; then remove mounting nuts
and lift the carburetor without damaging the exposed turbine wheel.
For overhaul, begin with inlet screen, cover, float/needle, pump
links, metering rod and jet in the order shown in Figure 9-35.

<a id="p730"></a> **[PDF p.730](../page-images/p0730.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-18 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Figure 9-35 identifies the Carter YH choke coil and piston, metering
rod, diaphragm accelerator pump, float and throttle assembly.
Complete disassembly only to the extent needed. Wash ordinary metal
parts in carburetor cleaner, but clean choke-coil housing separately
in gasoline and avoid soaking the pump. Check worn link holes,
throttle/choke plate and shafts, float leakage, sticking choke
piston, burrs on the needle/seat, and damaged metering rod/jet.
Replace needle and seat **together** if worn.

<a id="p731"></a> **[PDF p.731](../page-images/p0731.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-19 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Inspect flange mating faces for warpage and the pump diaphragm for
damage. Reassemble removed shafts with centered plates and new
retaining screws secured as shown. Use a new vacuum-passage O-ring
at choke housing. Fit diaphragm pump, spring, metering jet **without
a gasket**, splash shield, metering rod and link in Figure 9-37 order.
Do not bend or score the metering rod or jet.

<a id="p732"></a> **[PDF p.732](../page-images/p0732.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-20 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

To set the Carter metering rod, hold throttle shut, substitute
J-21056 gauge in jet, depress pump diaphragm until metering arm
touches its lifter link, then bend the metering arm so its pin just
contacts the gauge top. Install new float needle/seat and set
level/drop before refitting cover. Set fast idle **before** unloader:
with choke closed and cam on high step, a **.030-in.** gauge should
just pass between throttle plate and bore opposite idle port.
With throttle wide open and tension applied opposite the choke,
measure the unloader gap between choke edge and bore opposite the
vent-tube side: **7⁄16 in.** Bend the fast-idle cam's unloader tang
as needed.

<a id="p733"></a> **[PDF p.733](../page-images/p0733.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-21 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

The Carter YH assembly sequence says to set its choke-coil housing
**one notch rich**, inconsistent with the on-car instruction on p.726
and the later factory confirmation. Use the **one-notch-lean final
setting** above for the 1963 Spyder after assembly. Install the
carburetor with new air-cleaner O-ring, reconnect heat/fuel/linkage,
then set idle and optional throttle-return check. For charger
removal, take off spare tire and air cleaner, disconnect fuel/choke
heat, heat shield, oil lines and linkage, support the unit, and free
the turbine-housing V-clamp. Lift charger and carburetor together,
protecting turbine wheel and keeping fuel from spilling into engine.
Cover every exposed charger opening during other work.

<a id="p734"></a> **[PDF p.734](../page-images/p0734.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-22 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Overhaul the charger in a clean, dust-free work area. Remove the
compressor housing and gasket. Its impeller shaft nut has **left-hand
threads**: turn clockwise to remove. Protect the turbine wheel with
a cloth and support ring J-21004 while pressing shaft from impeller.
Remove sleeve, shims, turbine shield, spring ring, oil seal/mating
ring and bearing/retainer in the source order. Wash parts with diesel
oil or kerosene and a nylon brush.

<a id="p735"></a> **[PDF p.735](../page-images/p0735.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-23 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Do not use caustic cleaner or wire brushes on precision parts.
Inspect turbine and compressor housings for scoring, cracks and
gasket damage; blades for cracks, distortion or rubbing; shaft and
bearing for heat, carbon and wear; turbine shield/spring for damage;
and bearing-housing seal seats and snap-ring grooves. Replace
unserviceable pieces. Figure 9-47 identifies each rotating and
sealing component in the [exploded view](../diagrams/p0735-spyder-turbo-exploded.png).

<a id="p736"></a> **[PDF p.736](../page-images/p0736.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-24 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Inspect mating ring and floating carbon seal, then assemble with
new gaskets/O-rings in a clean area. Measure shaft-to-bearing play
and bearing-to-housing play separately. Shim bearing-to-housing to
**.001–.002 in.**, then add shaft-to-bearing play to obtain total
shaft end play; the factory example combines **.004 + .001 =
.005 in.** Available bearing shims are **.008, .009, .010, .011,
.012 and .014 in.** Use the dial-indicator setup in Figures 9-48
and 9-49 before selecting a shim.

<a id="p737"></a> **[PDF p.737](../page-images/p0737.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-25 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Install the mating ring, silicone-greased O-ring and oil seal/retainer.
For impeller shim choice, temporarily assemble shaft sleeve,
impeller, gasket and compressor housing; torque three alternate
housing bolts to **80 in-lb**. Lift impeller and read movement with
a dial indicator, then subtract total shaft end play. Select a
**.010- or .015-in.** shim as needed for final impeller-to-housing
clearance of **.015–.020 in.** The factory example is **.037 −
.005 = .032 in.** before shim selection.

<a id="p738"></a> **[PDF p.738](../page-images/p0738.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-26 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Install spring ring and turbine shield; oil shaft-seal groove and
compress its ring for insertion using tag wire or plastic installer.
Keep turbine wheel pressed against shield so seal cannot escape.
Install sleeve, selected impeller shim and impeller. Press impeller
fully onto shaft, or heat it to **no more than 300°F** for hand
installation. Position special washer dished side upward; start
the **left-hand-thread** self-locking nut.

<a id="p739"></a> **[PDF p.739](../page-images/p0739.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-27 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

Torque impeller nut to **80 in-lb** and six compressor-housing bolts
to **80 in-lb**. Before installation, pour oil into inlet until it
flows from drain; cover openings if assembly waits. If replacing
turbine housing, transfer choke heat tubes, use new inlet and outlet
gaskets, fit four inlet and three outlet flange nuts, and torque
them to **80 in-lb**.

<a id="p740"></a> **[PDF p.740](../page-images/p0740.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-28 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

To install charger/carburetor assembly, protect turbine wheel and
gasket, seat assembly at turbine housing, align induction tube and
oil lines, then torque V-clamp nut to **30–40 in-lb**. Reconnect
cross-tube hose, oil feed/drain, accelerator linkage, fuel line,
choke heat tube and air cleaner; set linkage. For a replacement
charger, install turbine housing separately with transferred choke
heat tubes before fitting the charger assembly.

<a id="p741"></a> **[PDF p.741](../page-images/p0741.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-29 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

The Spyder exhaust includes regular manifold pipes plus a turbine
inlet pipe, turbine outlet pipe, shield insulator, muffler, separate
tailpipe and turbine heat shield in the [factory component figure](../diagrams/p0741-spyder-exhaust-components.png).
For heat-shield removal, remove
spare tire, two compressor-housing bolts, and loosen outlet-pipe
clamp, then slide out shield leg. Muffler replacement follows regular
method but remove/reuse its **separate tailpipe** as applicable.
For exhaust-pipe service, support vehicle, free both manifold
flanges and turbine-inlet clamp, then work the pipe out; reinstall
with a new inlet clamp and manifold flanges positioned before
sliding the left pipe under transaxle.

<a id="p742"></a> **[PDF p.742](../page-images/p0742.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=9-30 years=1962,1963 scope=passenger variant=spyder -->

For turbine **outlet** pipe, support vehicle, remove muffler/tailpipe,
spare tire and heat shield, then free three turbine-flange nuts and
twist the pipe out through engine shield. Refit with a new outlet
gasket and muffler clamp. For turbine **inlet** pipe, remove engine
exhaust pipe, loosen muffler band, remove shield, disconnect charger
oil and intake/fuel/linkage, free four inlet and three outlet nuts,
lift charger/carburetor assembly, then remove inlet pipe/support
up through engine-side shield. Reverse with new inlet/outlet gaskets
and a new exhaust-to-inlet clamp.
