# Manual Transmissions — 1963 Passenger Cars

The 1963 passenger car could have a three-speed or optional four-speed
manual transmission. The 1962–63 supplement carries forward the 1961
service procedures but changes the linkage settings for both types and
identifies revised four-speed internal parts. Select the procedure for the
transmission and passenger model; **1200-series commercial** linkage is
excluded. Original files, hashes and combined PDF page numbers are in the
[source inventory](../data/source-inventory.json).

## Three-speed layout, lubrication, and in-car controls

<a id="p347"></a> **[PDF p.347](../page-images/p0347.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-1 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The three-speed is a synchromesh unit mounted to the differential carrier.
Its hollow mainshaft lets the clutch shaft pass forward. A single shift
rod has a finger that selects either the first/reverse fork or the
second/third fork; an interlock holds the unselected fork at neutral.
The base page's ratio and axle pairing examples describe 1961 production;
use 1963 Section 12 for year-specific gear/axle combinations.

<a id="p348"></a> **[PDF p.348](../page-images/p0348.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-2 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The factory cross-section locates the clutch gear, countergear and needle
bearings, mainshaft, first/reverse sliding gear, second/third clutch and
synchronizer, reverse idler, and both shift forks. Use the drawing to keep
bearings, thrust washers and forks in assembly order.

<a id="p349"></a> **[PDF p.349](../page-images/p0349.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-3 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The manual transaxle and differential carrier share gear lubricant but
retain separate drains and filler plugs. Check level at the carrier first;
if low, replenish it and check the transmission opening separately. Drain
and fill **both** units at the applicable 1963 service interval in Chapter
2. Do not use lubricant containing **active sulphur** or plain mineral
oil. The base page's **1961 total refill** and seat-contact linkage setting
are not used here; the supplement's 1963 linkage settings below govern.

The gearshift lever and tunnel rod can be serviced in the car. Internal
gear/bearing service requires removing the power train and separating
the transmission as Section 6 directs.

<a id="p350"></a> **[PDF p.350](../page-images/p0350.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-4 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

To service the floor lever, remove tunnel front cover and its four floor
nuts, then lift the assembly clear. The knob, retainer, spring, seat and
spherical joint separate within the lever housing. Inspect moving surfaces
for roughness, replace damaged parts, lubricate contacts, and install the
seat with its tab to the **right** before compressing/turning the retainer
into its housing lugs. For tunnel shift-rod removal, uncover the rear
boot, remove coupling cotter/pin, slide the coupling forward off the
transmission shifter, and release the front support bracket. If only the
coupling is replaced, scribe its position on the rod before removal.
The support-bracket **nylon** bushing described on this base page was
replaced by **Delrin** in the supplement.

<a id="p351"></a> **[PDF p.351](../page-images/p0351.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-5 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Refit the control rod with its lubricated ball socket engaged on the
gearshift-lever ball. Mount the front bracket, join and pin the rear
coupling, restore its boot and tunnel covers, then apply the **1963**
linkage adjustment below. For internal work, mount the removed
transmission securely, remove its front cover, and expand the clutch-
gear bearing snap ring with the factory puller before drawing the clutch
gear and bearing from the case.

<a id="p352"></a> **[PDF p.352](../page-images/p0352.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-6 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Center the snap-ring ends in the case recess **before expanding** them;
otherwise the ring can snag in the bearing groove and break the case.
As the clutch gear comes out, catch its loose needle rollers. Remove top
cover, mainshaft snap ring and mainshaft, then lift out thrust washer,
second gear, sliding gear and second/third clutch. Expand the rear
bearing snap ring to remove that bearing. Drive the countershaft out
without losing its needle bearings. If servicing reverse, remove its
idler lock pin and shaft, bearing and rear thrust washer.

<a id="p353"></a> **[PDF p.353](../page-images/p0353.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-7 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Remove shift-fork shafts, detent balls/springs and interlock in their
sequence, then the shift finger and manual rod. Clean bearings without
letting compressed air spin them; oil and turn each by hand to feel for
roughness. Check the case for cracks/burrs, gear teeth and sliding parts
for wear and free motion. The reverse-idler bushings are **not serviced
separately**; replace the gear if their shaft clearance is outside
**.002–.004 in.** Inspect all countergear needles and replace a worn set
with its shaft as directed by the factory.

| Three-speed reverse-idler bushing-to-shaft clearance | Factory value |
|---|---|
| Service fit | .002–.004 in. |

## Three-speed synchronizer and reassembly

<a id="p354"></a> **[PDF p.354](../page-images/p0354.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-8 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

To replace the clutch-gear bearing, support its outer race and tap the
gear out with a soft hammer; press the new bearing on through its **inner
race**. For the second/third synchronizer, remove the first/reverse
sliding gear, turn each synchronizer ring until its retainer ends show
through the sleeve slot, and expand the retainer with J-0932. Inspect
ring-to-cone fit, surface smoothness and sleeve end play. If cone-to-snap-
ring end play exceeds **.030 in.**, replace the clutch sleeve and both
synchronizer rings.

<a id="p355"></a> **[PDF p.355](../page-images/p0355.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-9 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Press each ring onto its cone by hand to check for rocking; a loose rock
prevents proper synchronization. Oil rings and seat their retainers fully
so rings turn freely, then reinstall first/reverse sliding gear. Fit each
energizing spring with its **offset locking end between the third and
fourth teeth** of a gear-tooth bank as Figure 6D-16 shows. Replace any
removed energizing spring and avoid stretching it beyond gear-tooth
diameter during installation.

<a id="p356"></a> **[PDF p.356](../page-images/p0356.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-10 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Oil the manual control shaft and refit its shift finger and locked screws.
Install detent springs/balls and first/reverse fork, then interlock and
second/third fork with the shaft groove correctly engaging the interlock;
secure both forks with their roll pins. Use grease to hold **25 needle
rollers at each end** of the countergear. Grease its thrust washers with
tabs aligned to the case notches, use J-5777 as an assembly shaft, and
drive the permanent countershaft through while displacing the tool.

<a id="p357"></a> **[PDF p.357](../page-images/p0357.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-11 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Seat/stake the countershaft as shown. Fit reverse-idler thrust washer and
needle bearing at the **rear, chamfered-tooth end** of its gear; align the
idler-shaft lock-pin hole to the case and fit a **new** lock pin. Install
the mainshaft rear bearing and fully seat its retainer. Assemble the
first/reverse slider, second/third clutch and second gear in the case;
insert the mainshaft from the front, install the thrust washer with its
**oil grooves toward the gear**, and lock the rear snap ring.

<a id="p358"></a> **[PDF p.358](../page-images/p0358.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-12 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Check mainshaft rear snap-ring-to-bearing end clearance with a feeler;
it must be **.004 in. maximum**. Select a different thrust washer if
needed. Grease and place clutch-gear pilot rollers with their small and
large spacers in the illustrated order, then align synchronizer lands to
clutch-gear blank teeth. Seat the clutch-gear bearing and its retainer
fully, install front/top covers with gaskets, and restore case expansion
plugs.

| Three-speed assembled mainshaft | Factory limit |
|---|---|
| Rear snap-ring-to-bearing end clearance | .004 in. maximum |

<a id="p359"></a> **[PDF p.359](../page-images/p0359.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-13 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

If the transmission jumps out of third, check carrier mounting and clean
case mating faces, then the **1963 linkage adjustment**, fork travel,
clutch-gear bearing/retainer and mainshaft pilot. For low/reverse jump-out,
inspect partial engagement damage, sleeve/slider splines and linkage.
For noise, separate all-gear lubricant/countergear faults from high-only
mainshaft or clutch-gear bearings and neutral-running clutch-gear/pilot
bearings.

<a id="p360"></a> **[PDF p.360](../page-images/p0360.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-14 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Second-only noise points to its constant-mesh gear or rear countergear
bearings; low/reverse noise points to sliding/countergear teeth; reverse-
only noise points to its idler and bushings. Check the second-gear thrust
washer and rear bearing for second-gear backlash. For leaks, check
overfill, covers/gaskets, shift-shaft seal, idler expansion plugs and
countershaft fit. Use **1963 Section 12** specifications for ratio and
capacity lookup.

## Four-speed layout and removal

<a id="p361"></a> **[PDF p.361](../page-images/p0361.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-15 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The optional four-speed has constant-mesh helical gears and synchronizers
for **all forward speeds**; reverse is unsynchronized. Its hollow
mainshaft carries the clutch shaft through to the clutch gear. A shift
finger selects the 1–2, 3–4 or reverse fork. The spring-loaded reverse
inhibitor resists accidental crossover into reverse. The 1961 gear ratios
on this page are not used as 1963 specifications; consult 1963 Section 12.

<a id="p362"></a> **[PDF p.362](../page-images/p0362.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-16 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The [factory cross-section](../diagrams/p0362-four-speed-cross-section.png)
identifies the two synchronizer assemblies, blocker rings, reverse idler,
countergear, thrust washers, shift forks and interlocks. Its labeled
clutch-gear roller count describes the original assembly; observe the
1962–63 revised-mainshaft compatibility rule below when replacing parts.

<a id="p363"></a> **[PDF p.363](../page-images/p0363.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-17 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Four-speed and differential carrier use common SAE 80 multipurpose gear
lubricant, with separate fill and drain openings. Check carrier level;
if it was low, replenish and also check the transmission. Drain and refill
both at the applicable Chapter 2 interval. Avoid lubricant containing
active sulphur and plain mineral oil. Apply the **1963 four-speed linkage
setting below** in place of this page's seat-contact setting. Floor-lever
and tunnel-rod service follows the three-speed procedure, except the
four-speed lever has **no separate seat piece**. Remove the power train
and separate the transmission for internal work.

<a id="p364"></a> **[PDF p.364](../page-images/p0364.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-18 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

For teardown, remove front and side covers. Remove the 3–4 detent plug,
spring and ball, shift into fourth, drive out the fork roll pin and remove
its shaft/fork. Repeat for the 1–2 fork while shifted fully into second.
Remove the three clutch-gear/bearing retaining snap rings in the order
shown. Seat puller J-8880's plates in the bearing groove, secure them,
insert its bore adapter and draw the clutch gear bearing out with the
puller body and screw.

<a id="p365"></a> **[PDF p.365](../page-images/p0365.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-19 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Lift the mainshaft/gears/rear bearing retainer together, keeping the
clutch gear on the shaft; then put both synchronizers in neutral. Remove
reverse shifter head and shaft, reverse idler shaft and Woodruff key,
idler gear, fork and lever. Drive the countershaft out with a **7 in.
dummy shaft** of the diameter illustrated by the factory; keep the dummy
inside the countergear so its needles stay captured. Collect both thrust
washers, interlocks and detent parts, then remove the shift finger/selector
shaft and inhibitor. The supplement changes the inhibitor design; identify
the installed version before replacement. For mainshaft teardown, stand
the clutch gear with pilot bore upward, withdraw the mainshaft carefully,
remove its front special snap ring, then slide off the 3–4 synchronizer,
blocker rings, third gear, radial needle bearing and second gear.

## Four-speed inspection and reassembly

<a id="p366"></a> **[PDF p.366](../page-images/p0366.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-20 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Remove the rear selective snap ring and the bearing/retainer, first-gear
thrust washer and gear, and 1–2 blocker rings. Press the shaft from its
1–2 synchronizer and first-gear sleeve. Clean the case and inspect its
carrier mating face for burrs, its seal, bearings, rollers, spacers,
gears and thrust washers for wear. **Do not spin bearings with compressed
air**; oil and turn them slowly by hand. Replace worn rollers and mating
shaft parts as a compatible set.

<a id="p367"></a> **[PDF p.367](../page-images/p0367.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-21 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Synchronizer hub and sliding sleeve are a **matched assembly**; keep
their etched alignment marks together. Keys and springs can be replaced.
Put the two springs on opposite hub sides with their tanged ends in the
same keyway, hold keys in place and slide the hub into its original sleeve.
Press a replacement rear bearing into its retainer with the retainer ring
expanded. Press the 1–2 hub and first-gear sleeve to their seats on the
mainshaft. Engage the **longer-hub 1–2 blocker ring** with synchronizer
keys, fit first gear and thrust washer, and retain the assembled rear
bearing with a selective snap ring. Choose among the factory's three
ring thicknesses for **.005 in. maximum** rear-bearing-to-ring end play.

<a id="p368"></a> **[PDF p.368](../page-images/p0368.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-22 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Invert the mainshaft and fit the other long-hub 1–2 blocker ring. Install
second gear with clutching teeth toward the 1–2 synchronizer, its radial
needle bearing, then third gear with clutching teeth upward. Fit the
shorter-hub 3–4 blocker ring to third gear, followed by the 3–4
synchronizer and its other blocker ring; engage each ring's notches with
the keys. Grease compatible clutch-gear rollers into their races before
sliding the clutch gear over the mainshaft. The supplement requires a
revised gear and matching needles with its revised mainshaft; identify
the installed parts before assembly. Chevrolet's later [*1960–69 Corvair
Parts & Accessories Catalog*, groups 4.351–4.352, printed p. 4-12](https://northern-california-corvairs.com/1960-1969-Corvair-Parts-Catalog.pdf#page=204)
lists the 1961–63 four-speed clutch gear as part **3814014** and the
1961–66 four-speed mainshaft rollers as part **7451240**, quantity **72**.
That is a **total service-parts count**, not an inner/outer split. The
base procedure's 33-inner / 37-outer placement totals only 70 and must
not be applied to the revised gear without identifying the installed
gear and rollers. The catalog gives no distribution between its two
races.

<a id="p369"></a> **[PDF p.369](../page-images/p0369.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-23 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Fit the correct reverse inhibitor and plunger on their dowel. Grease the
selector shaft and insert it **from inside the case** to protect the seal
lips; lock the shift finger bolts and replace the rear plug. Petroleum
jelly holds the countergear thrust washers with tabs in case grooves.
Raise the countergear with its dummy shaft, drive the countershaft from
the rear while pushing the dummy out, and seat the permanent shaft flush
at the rear. Fit reverse shifter lever and fork, slide the idler gear/shaft
into position with the Woodruff key engaged, then seat and stake the idler
and fork shaft bores **below** the carrier mating face.

<a id="p370"></a> **[PDF p.370](../page-images/p0370.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-24 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Install the 3–4 detent ball and spring and identify the three fork shafts
by the factory notch/pin-hole drawing. Fit reverse shifter head and shaft
with its detent compressed and roll-pin the head. Move both synchronizers
fully forward into **second and fourth** for mainshaft insertion past
the countergear; seat the rear bearing retainer flush. Fit front bearing
and its case snap ring. Select the clutch-gear bearing snap ring for
**.005 in. maximum** bearing-to-ring end play, then fit the small
clutch-shaft bottoming snap ring. Return both synchronizers to neutral
before installing interlocks and the 1–2 fork.

<a id="p371"></a> **[PDF p.371](../page-images/p0371.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-25 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

Install the 1–2 shaft with its opposed interlock notches rearward;
engage the fork's through-gate with the synchronizer and secure its roll
pin. Fit its detent with the **long-shank** cap. Drop in the remaining
interlock, fit and roll-pin the 3–4 fork/shaft, and install the remaining
3–4 detent, nylon washer and cap. Operate the selector through every
range before closing the case. Install the front bearing retainer with a
new gasket and seven bolts at **15–20 ft-lb**; install the side cover
with a new gasket and eight bolts at **3–4 ft-lb**.

<a id="p372"></a> **[PDF p.372](../page-images/p0372.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-26 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

For high-gear jump-out, check transmission-to-carrier fastening, clean
mating faces, linkage movement/full engagement, clutch-gear bearing and
retainer, and mainshaft pilot. For all-gear noise, check lubricant,
countergear bearings/shaft, gears and main bearings; for high-only or
neutral-running noise, isolate the clutch-gear/mainshaft bearings.
Second-only noise points to second constant-mesh gears or countergear
bearings; low/reverse noise to their teeth; reverse-only noise to idler
gear/bushings. Leaks call for level, covers/gaskets, shifter seal and
countershaft-fit checks. Use the 1963 linkage setting below.

<a id="p373"></a> **[PDF p.373](../page-images/p0373.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-27 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=commercial; excluded=passenger -->

This is a **Corvair 95 and Greenbrier 1200 commercial** page. Its
different linkage and gear ratios are excluded from the passenger-car
reference. The page points to Section 12 for transmission specifications.

<a id="p374"></a> **[PDF p.374](../page-images/p0374.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=base-1961 page=6D-28 years=1961,1962,1963 scope=passenger -->

The factory tool plate identifies the clutch-gear puller, synchronizer
retainer pliers, bearing tools, holding fixture and torque wrench. Match
tool numbers against the original plate when preparing an overhaul.

## 1963 three-speed linkage and support

<a id="p676"></a> **[PDF p.676](../page-images/p0676.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=6D-1 years=1963 scope=passenger -->

The three-speed shift-control-rod support bracket is located as Figure
6D-1 shows and has a **Delrin bushing** in place of the earlier nylon
bushing. Adjust linkage after replacing the tunnel shift rod or when the
lever pattern fails to engage the expected range. Before setting length,
move the shift control rod **rearward to take up lash**.

For **500 and 700** models, move the seat fully forward, select **first
gear**, loosen the transmission shift-control-rod coupling nut, and set
the shift lever **½ in. from the seat edge**. Tighten the coupling clamp
nut and test every range. For a **900** model, use the same method but
select **reverse** and set the lever knob center **2¼ in. rearward of the
shift-housing center**. The factory gearshift support/bracket drawing is
shown in the [1963 linkage illustration](../diagrams/p0676-gearshift-support.png).

| 1963 three-speed passenger linkage | Selected gear | Lever setting |
|---|---|---|
| 500/700 | First | ½ in. from seat edge, seat fully forward |
| 900 | Reverse | Knob center 2¼ in. rearward of housing center |

## 1963 four-speed part compatibility and linkage

The four-speed's reverse inhibitor was redesigned. The mainshaft distance
between its shoulder and third/fourth-speed snap-ring groove increased;
the revised shaft requires the **revised clutch gear and matching needle
bearings**. The new gear/bearings may be used with a 1961 shaft, but a
replacement new-design shaft in an older transmission must be paired with
them. Keep the three parts as a compatible set rather than reusing the
earlier gear/bearings on the revised shaft.

<a id="p677"></a> **[PDF p.677](../page-images/p0677.png)** <!-- SOURCE id=supplement-1962-63 page=6D-2 years=1963 scope=passenger -->

For **500 and 700** four-speed linkage adjustment, take up rod lash
rearward, move the seat fully forward, select **fourth gear**, loosen the
coupling clamp, and set the lever **½ in. from the seat edge**. Tighten
the clamp and test all ranges. For a **900**, select **reverse** instead;
set knob center **3¼ in. rearward of shift-housing center**, then tighten
and test. The remaining 1200-series linkage drawing and gauge-block
setting on this page concern commercial vehicles and are excluded.

| 1963 four-speed passenger linkage | Selected gear | Lever setting |
|---|---|---|
| 500/700 | Fourth | ½ in. from seat edge, seat fully forward |
| 900 | Reverse | Knob center 3¼ in. rearward of housing center |
