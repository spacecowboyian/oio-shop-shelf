# Factory repair manual, 1st edition October 1970 — title page and contents

This part of the PDF (PDF pp.375-433) is a separate document from the large Reparaturhandbuch
that occupies PDF pp.1-374. It is paginated by **section codes** printed in the page headers
(`A-1`, `A-2`, `B-2` … `N-5`, `R-…`), not by continuous page numbers. Much of its data is also
printed in the large manual; where the two printings disagree, the chapters of this part say so.
