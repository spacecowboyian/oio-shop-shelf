## Wiring diagram — Alpine 1300 (*Schaltschema*, BERLINETTE A. 110, 30 June 1970)

The diagram is a landscape fold-out for the **Alpine 1300** only, dated **30 JUNI 70**. Its
component labels are printed in French (e.g. *batterie*, *démarreur*, *dynamo*, *régulateur*,
*bobine*, *allumeur*, *centrale clignotante*, *essuie-glace*, *plafonnier*). It is delivered as an
image; wire routes cannot be faithfully transcribed as text.
