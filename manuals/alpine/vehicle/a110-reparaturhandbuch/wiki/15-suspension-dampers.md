## Removing and refitting a front damper

### Removal

1. Raise the front of the car and remove the wheel concerned.
2. Slacken the upper securing nut.
3. Remove the nuts of the lower damper bracket.
4. Withdraw the damper downwards.
5. Separate the bracket from the damper.

### Refitting

1. Fit the lower damper bracket **(1)**.
2. Pull out the piston rod; push the rubber sleeve **(2)** onto the upper threaded piece.
3. Insert the damper from below and secure the bracket.
4. Push the rubber bush **(3)** onto the upper threaded rod so that the nose of the sleeve
   engages in the cup of the cross-member.
5. Fit the dished washer **(4)**.
6. Lock the nut **(5)** and the locknut **(6)**.

## Removing and refitting a rear damper

### Removal

1. Slacken the wheel nuts.
2. Raise the rear of the car and remove the wheel concerned.
3. Release the upper and lower damper mountings.
4. Remove the damper.

### Refitting

Carry out all the removal operations in reverse order.

## Replacing a bump-stop rubber

1. Release the securing bolt of the bump-stop rubber.
2. Separate the rubber from the mounting plate.
3. Fit the new rubber to the plate.
4. Refit the mounting plate with the bump-stop rubber.

## Removing and refitting a front spring

### Removal

1. Remove the damper and fit the spring compressor **Sus.20**.
2. Remove the anti-roll bar.
3. Release the lower ball joint of the stub-axle carrier with the tool **T.Av.55**.
4. Release the spring compressor.
5. Push the wishbone down.
6. Remove the spring.

<!-- NEEDS REVIEW: printed "Torsionsstab" (torsion bar); on this front axle the only bar is the
anti-roll bar (Querstabilisator), so it is rendered "anti-roll bar". -->

### Refitting

Carry out all the removal operations in reverse order. Set the height of the track-rod ball pins
and check the toe-in (see [Front axle](13-front-axle.md)).

## Removing and refitting a rear spring

### Removal

1. Slacken the wheel nuts and raise the rear of the car.
2. Remove the wheel concerned.
3. Release the handbrake cable.
4. Unlock the locknut of the handbrake adjusting screw and unscrew it as far as possible.
5. Slacken the handbrake adjusting screw a few turns.
6. Free the end of the guide plate from the dowel pin.
7. Remove the brake caliper (**do not disconnect the brake hose**).
8. Compress the spring with the compressor **Sus.21**.
9. Release the upper mounting of the dampers.
10. Release one side of the check strap.
11. Remove the spring with the rubber seat.

### Refitting

Carry out all the removal operations in reverse order.
