## E-4 — Pinion setting dimension, removal and overhaul

The figure (65 083) shows a gearbox section with the **conical distance A** dimensioned from the
pinion to the differential axis.

![Gearbox section showing the conical distance (pinion setting dimension) A — PDF p.410](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/releases/download/manuals-a110-reparaturhandbuch/p0410-gearbox-conical-distance-a.png)

> **NOTE:** the speedometer is driven by a square drive, since there is no transmission angle as
> on the RENAULT 8 GORDINI. A **"Hewland"** limited-slip differential (*Differentialsperre*) is
> available exclusively for the racing version.

### Removing and refitting the gearbox

The general removal operations are the same as for the Renault 8 and Renault 10 series.

- **Alpine 1300 – 1300 G – 1300 S:** on these vehicles the gearbox can be removed on its own.
- **Alpine 1600:** it is recommended to remove the complete power unit.

> **NOTE:** when refitting, do not forget to fit the shims between the side and centre rubber
> mountings of the gearbox suspension.

### Overhauling the gearbox

The repair operations and the setting values are the same as for gearbox types 330-00 and 353-13
respectively; however, the conical distance given in the table must be taken into account. For
gearbox types 330-06 and 330-08 see **MR 131**; for gearbox types 353 and 364 see **MR 133
(R. 1135)**.

### Replacing the gear linkage or the gear lever

1. Remove the gaiter and the cover housing of the gear lever.
2. Release the linkage at the gearbox and take it out, together with the gear lever, towards the
   rear and downwards.
3. Separate the linkage and the lever.

If there are difficulties in removing the linkage, slacken the rear engine mounting so that the
gearbox can be raised.
