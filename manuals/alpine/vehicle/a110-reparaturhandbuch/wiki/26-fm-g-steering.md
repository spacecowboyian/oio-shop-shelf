## G-1 — Steering: longitudinal section and data

The page opens with a longitudinal section of the rack (figure 62 674), without values.

Rack-and-pinion steering with a compensating spring (*Ausgleichfeder*).

| | Standard | On request: direct steering |
|---|---|---|
| Pinion | 7 teeth | 9 teeth |
| Ratio (*Übersetzung*) | 17.1 | 13.5 |
| Turns of the steering wheel | 3.2 | 2.5 |
| Rack travel per turn of the steering wheel | 44.8 mm | 57.5 mm |

This steering can be overhauled. When a pinion with **9 teeth** is fitted instead of 7, the rack
bushes must also be replaced (the rack is then positioned **2 mm higher**).

### Removing, overhauling and refitting the steering

The work is the same as on the RENAULT 8 — see **MR 68**.

> **NOTE — MOUNTING.** Two types of mounting are used:
>
> 1. **Brackets welded to the crossmember:** the mounting holes for the steering are slightly
>    elongated (*ovalisiert*).
> 2. **Brackets bolted to the crossmember:** two different brackets can be fitted — the standard
>    bracket (mounting holes at a height of **H = 55 mm**) and brackets for raised steering
>    (**H = 56 or 57 mm**); the height is stamped on the rear face of the bracket.
>
> After fitting the steering, the change in toe between the laden and unladen vehicle **must**
> be checked: see [Section H — Front axle](27-fm-h-front-axle.md).

The lower figure shows the steering rack on the front crossmember with the bracket height **H**
marked.
