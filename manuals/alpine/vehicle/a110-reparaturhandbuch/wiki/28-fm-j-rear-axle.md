## J-2 — Rear axle: modifications for negative camber

Just as on the front axle, modifications have been made here to obtain **negative camber**. They
are as follows.

### Alpine 1300 and 1300 G – 1600 S: normal version with gearbox type 353

- Fitting of **12 mm** wedges (*Keile*) between the crossmember and the side gearbox mountings.
- Fitting of **12 mm** wedges between the gearbox and the centre gearbox mounting bracket; this
  also requires replacement of the bolts.

> **NOTE:** **6 mm** wedges for the Alpine 1600 S with gearbox type **364** and hard side rubber
> mountings (rubber silentbloc set no. **0996128200**).

### Alpine 1300 G (works assembly) – 1300 S

- Fitting of **23 mm** wedges between the crossmember and the side gearbox mountings.
- Replacement of the centre mounting bracket (ALPINE bracket).
- Fitting of a strut between the clutch housing and the crossmember.
- Side rubber mountings of greater hardness (rubber silentbloc set no. **0996128200**).

The axle-locating struts (*Achsführungsstreben*) have also been modified: on the chassis side
they are fitted with two toothed pieces (*Zahnstücke*) instead of the rubber bushes.

On request, struts can be fitted between the front engine crossmember and the cylinder block; in
this case a crossmember *(text cut off at the foot of the page)* … with welded-on threaded pieces
must be fitted. All these modifications are intended to stiffen the rear axle and so give better
stability of the vehicle at high speeds.

### Removing, overhauling and refitting the rear axle or replacing individual parts

See **MR 133, chapter J**.

The axle tubes are fitted **without preload**. The eccentrics therefore need not be adjusted to
obtain toe-in or toe-out at the rear wheels.
