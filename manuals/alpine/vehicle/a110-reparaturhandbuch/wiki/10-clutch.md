## Setting the clutch free play

### Engine 807/843/844

The release lever is on the intake side of the engine, below the large V-belt pulley.

1. Slacken the locknut **(1)**.
2. With the adjusting nut **(2)**, set the prescribed play of **2 - 3 mm**.
3. Retighten the locknut **(1)**.

### Engine 810/812

The adjustment is made on the underside of the vehicle, below the gearbox.

1. Slacken the locknut **(1)**.
2. Slacken or tighten the nut **(2)** until the play at the release fork is **2 to 3 mm**.
3. Tighten the locknut.

## Release bearing (engine 807/843/844) — replacement

### Release bearing for a clutch mechanism with release plate

#### Removal

1. Extract the roll pins of the release fork:
   - with tool **Emb.322** for the cylindrical pins **A**;
   - with tool **Emb.384** for the grooved pins **B**.
2. Remove the release shaft from the release fork and pull the release bearing with the fork off
   the guide.

#### Fitting

1. Lubricate the guide with **Molykote BR 2**.
2. Fit the new bearing into the fork and slide it onto the guide.
3. Insert the release shaft and align it with the fork.
4. Insert the pins, observing the protrusion **D** relative to the release fork: **D = 1 mm**.

![Release fork pins A (cylindrical) and B (grooved), and pin protrusion D = 1 mm — PDF p.164](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/releases/download/manuals-a110-reparaturhandbuch/p0164-release-fork-pins-protrusion-d.png)

### Release bearing for a clutch mechanism without release plate (engine 807/843/844)

1. Remove the spring from the release bearing and release fork.
2. Pull the release bearing off the guide.
3. Lubricate the release bearing guide and the bearing faces of the release fork with
   **Molykote BR 2** grease.
4. Slide on the new release bearing.
5. Insert the ends of the retaining springs into the holes of the bearing carrier and release
   fork.
6. Lubricate the face of the release bearing that bears on the diaphragm spring with
   **Molykote BR 2**.

## Release fork (engine 807/843/844) — replacement

### Clutch mechanism with release plate

#### Removal

1. Extract the roll pins of the release fork:
   - tool **Emb.322** for the cylindrical pins **A**;
   - tool **Emb.384** for the grooved pins **B**.
2. Separate the release fork from the release shaft and pull the release bearing with the fork
   off the guide.
3. Unhook the return spring.
4. Remove the release shaft.

### Clutch mechanism without release plate (engine 807/843/844)

#### Removal

1. Remove the release bearing.
2. Extract the roll pins of the release fork with tool **Emb.334**.
3. Pull out the release shaft; catch the fork and the spring.

#### Fitting

1. Lubricate the release shaft with **Molykote BR 2**.
2. Insert the release shaft and fit the fork with its spring.
3. Insert the roll pins, observing the protrusion **D** relative to the release fork:
   **D = 1 mm**.

## Replacing the release bearing and fork (engine 810/812)

### Replacing the release bearing

1. Free the spring of the bearing trunnions from its stop.
2. Lift one of the clamping eyes **(1)** and take out the release bearing.
3. Coat the trunnions of the new bearing with graphite grease and fit them.
4. Press the clamping eye closed again and check that the release bearing does not bind in the
   trunnions.
5. Refit the spring.

### Replacing the release fork

1. Unlock and remove the bolt **(1)**.
2. Remove the release fork with the thrust bearing and spring.
3. Remove the spring.
4. Lift the clamping eye **(2)** of the fork slightly and release the thrust bearing.
5. Lubricate the bearing trunnions of the thrust bearing with graphite grease and fit them into
   the new release fork.
6. Press the clamping eyes **(2)** together and check that the bearing does not bind.
7. Rehook the spring.
8. Lubricate the pressure dome of the fork on the housing (graphite grease).
9. Fit the fork.
10. Hook in the spring support **(3)**.
11. Lock the hollow bolt **(1)**.
12. Bend over the lock tab.

## Clutch shaft seal (all types) — replacement

1. Separate the clutch housing from the gearbox.
2. Press out the seal.
3. Fit:
   - the new seal;
   - the clutch housing to the gearbox.

> To avoid the seal lip being damaged by the splines of the clutch shaft, wrap the splined part
> with adhesive tape before offering up the clutch housing.

4. Refit the gearbox.

<a id="p170"></a>

## Clutch shaft spigot bearing (all types) — replacement

1. Remove the flywheel.
2. Extract the bearing with the puller **Mot.11**.
3. Fit the new bearing.

> The new bearing is already lubricated and provided with a grease reserve; it **must not be
> washed out**.
