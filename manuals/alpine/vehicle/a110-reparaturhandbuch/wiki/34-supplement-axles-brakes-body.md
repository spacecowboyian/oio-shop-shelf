### I — Front brakes: fitting instructions

#### a) Mounting of the brake carrier bracket

1. Fit the aluminium bracket to the steering knuckle (*Achsschenkel*) and — if necessary — file out the
   clearance of the upper or lower part of the steering knuckle.
2. Mount the bracket with four square-head bolts of **8 x 35** (order no. **07 06 302 900**) with nuts
   and washers.

#### b) Fitting the brake carrier

1. Fit the brake carrier and fasten it to its bracket with bolts of **12 x 30** and spring washers
   (*Fächerscheiben*).
2. For all further details see repair manual **MR 96**, chapter M.

#### c) Fitting the hub

1. On the modified front hub of type **R.1135** fit the spacer washer no. **60 00 001 387** and the

   thickness of the bolt heads: **6.8 cm**; also use nuts, washers, etc.

### II — Rear brakes: fitting instructions (with modified axle tube)

#### a) Brake carrier bracket and thrust-sleeve bracket

1. Fit the thrust-sleeve bracket (*Halterung der Druckhülse*) **RIGHT** or **LEFT**, no.
   **60 00 001 435** or **60 00 001 436** respectively, and the brake carrier bracket (symmetrical
   parts) **60 00 01 437**, using square-head bolts **8 x 35**, order no. **07 06 302 900**, to the
   modified axle tube.
2. Fit the bearing race with its seal; then fit the bearing, the drive shaft and the bolts.
3. If necessary saw off the smooth part of the retaining bolt no. **06 05 959 400** of the guide plate.
4. Place a flat washer of **3 mm** thickness on this bolt to compensate for the thickness of the
   thrust-sleeve bracket.
5. Fit the bearing cage, bearing and drive shaft together onto the axle tube.

#### b) Fitting the hub and the brake disc

1. On the modified hub of type **R.1135** fit the centring bolt no. **60 00 001 387** (recess towards
   the hub) and the rear brake disc (thickness **10 mm**) using **three bolts of 10 x 27**; height of the
   bolt heads: **4.2** — order no. **60 001 427**. Fit the assembly onto the axle shaft.

#### c) Fitting the brake carrier

1. Fit the brake carrier.
2. If necessary grind off the part that touches the raised portion of the bearing cage.
3. Fit the brake carrier using bolts **10 x 35**, order no. **60 00 001 444**, with spring washers.

#### d) Fitting the caliper

1. On the outside of the caliper, in the axis of the piston, drill a hole of **11 mm** diameter so that
   the piston can be pushed back through it (7 mm hexagon socket-head key).

## Chapter N — Checking the chassis (amends [31](31-fm-n-body.md); see also MR 131, chapter N)

### A — Checking the chassis

The following tools are required: **a)** the gauge **Car.27** (*Messlehre*); **b)** the body straightening
bench (*Karosserie-Richtbank*); **c)** certain tools of own manufacture.

#### 1 — Various uses of the gauge (see drawing ALPINE 05-11-04)

First check the position of the plumb disc (*Lotscheibe*) which is welded on below the central member.
This disc must be at a distance of **570 mm ±2** from the pedal-assembly axis. If necessary correct its
position, or drill a hole of **10.5 mm** diameter in the member at the point concerned and close it with
sealing compound.

**a) Checking the front axle: alignment of the lower wishbones**

1. Check the caster (*Nachlauf*) and the difference between right and left; maximum difference **1°**
   (an adjustment of 1° corresponds to an offset of the lower ball pin of **3 mm**).
2. Measure and compare the dimensions **E** and **E1** with the gauge fitted with the **short** probe tip
   (the measuring points on the front axle are the bulges of the bearing shells of the lower ball pins).

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| E | 1005 ±2 |
| E1 | 1008 ±2 |

   Since it is preferable that the caster on the right is slightly greater than on the left, E1 may be
   **3 mm** greater than E (see above).

**b) Checking the front axle: position of the upper wishbones and the track-rod ball pins**

1. Fit the gauge with the **angled** probe tip and compare the distances right and left (see MR 131,
   chapter N).

**c) Checking the front axle cross-member: with the front axle fitted or removed**

1. The measuring points to be checked are the axes or inner fixing bolts of the rear lower
   longitudinal arms, right and left. Compare: **A = A1 = 794 mm ±2**.

**d) Checking the rear axle: position of the axle tubes**

1. Taking into account the restrictions listed on page N-13 of MR 131: use the centre of the lower
   fixing bolt of the bearing cage as the measuring point; fit the gauge with the **short** probe tip
   and compare:

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| D = D1 | 1386 mm ±2 for all models except 1600 S |
| D = D1 | 1380 mm ±2 for 1600 S |

### C — Replacing the chassis

Correct replacement requires the use of the straightening bench with the special ALPINE mounting
blocks.

Order no. of the **complete chassis: 60 00 001 667**.

### D — Replacing the front cross-member

1. Remove: the front axle, the rear axle, the steering, the master cylinder.
2. Remove the fuel tank; if it is a steel tank, release the tank's retaining strap.
3. Disconnect the cooling-system pipes (when the radiator is mounted at the front).
4. Cut out the bulkhead (*Spritzwand*) — the area between the vertical axis of the front cross-member,
   the luggage-compartment floor and the wheel arches.
5. Separate the front cross-member from the central member, the pedal-assembly member and the
   longitudinal members with a cutting torch.
6. Separate it from the wheel arches and cut off the two longitudinal members as close as possible to
   the front cross-member.
7. Remove the front cross-member.

The spare-parts store supplies in exchange: the front cross-member; 2 front longitudinal members;
2 connecting plates; 2 hose holders; 2 brackets for the spring seats of the bump stops; 2 brackets for
the steering box.

8. Place the body on the straightening bench (mounting block for the rear-axle cross-member) and, with
   the aid of the wedges between the lower part of the central member and the bench, produce a
   distance of **137 ±1**, about **100 mm** behind the axis of the front members (this support is
   necessary because the front cross-member is removed).
9. Fit the holder for the anti-roll bar bearing blocks (05-11-03).
10. Place the front cross-member on the straightening bench fitted with the supports; insert it into
    the member.
11. If necessary fit the dimension **A = A1 = 794 ±2** (drawing 05-11-03).
12. Place the front cross-member on the straightening bench fitted with the supports; insert it into
    the member.
13. If necessary check the dimension **A = A1 = 794 ±2** (drawing 05-11-04).
14. Fit the brackets for the spring seats (bore in the axis of the cross-member).
15. Fit the front longitudinal members to the cross-member, at the level of the brackets for the spring
    seats.
16. Fit the connecting plates to the front longitudinal members so that their lower edge coincides with
    the luggage-compartment floor.

21. Put the cut-out part back into the bulkhead and glue it.
22. Reassemble the mechanical assemblies; set the front axle and the steering-box height.

### H — Sealing of windscreen and rear window

#### a) Series production

Since **May 1969** a number of improvements have been made:

**1/ Windscreen: sealing profile no. 60 00 000 138 — plate 82-30 of PR 871**

- Fitting of a moulded neoprene profile instead of the neoprene ring profile.
- Since **2-1-70** a sealing profile of natural rubber with a changed cross-section is fitted (see
  figure A); the latter has better elasticity.
- More favourable cross-section by enlarging the sealing lip seated in the groove and reducing the
  shoulder (see figure B).
- Since **10-2-70** (on bodies made in DIEPPE) and since **20-4-70** (on bodies made in THIRON-GARDAIS)
  drain holes are provided at the lower corners of the windscreen frame (see figure C); the electric
  cables of the windscreen heating, if fitted, are also led through these holes.

In addition, various measures are taken at the body line:

- Keeping to the width and profile of the frame.
- Reduction of excess thickness of polyester and sealing compound around the frame (figure E).
- Reduction of the thickness of the trim of the windscreen pillars at the connection with the frame.

**2/ Rear window: sealing profile no. 60 00 000 139 — plate 82-30 of PR 871**

- Fitting of a rubber moulded profile instead of the neoprene ring profile.
- Drain holes in the rear-window frame from the dates given above (figure D).
- The same measures at the body line as listed under a1.

#### b) In the case of repair

**1/ Windscreen**

1. Remove the windscreen and clean the frame.
2. With the aid of a sponge put some water into the frame profile to check whether — with the vehicle
   standing level — the point indicated on figure C is really the lowest point of the curve.
