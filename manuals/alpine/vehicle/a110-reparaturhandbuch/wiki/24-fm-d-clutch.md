> **NOTE:** a certain number of 1600 S vehicles were fitted with the **200 D clutch of type
> R. 1151**. The calibration value of the clutch mechanism is stamped on the cover between two
> fixing holes.

### Removing – overhauling – refitting the clutch

It is recommended to remove the engine when the clutch has to be overhauled.

## D-2 — Clutch 200 DEC, racing version (*Kupplung 200 DEC – Rennsportausführung*)

> **IMPORTANT:** with this type of clutch a noise may occur when the clutch is disengaged,
> similar to that caused by an insufficiently lubricated release bearing. This characteristic
> noise is normal and is due to a "vibration" of the pressure plate in its guides.
