## R-1 — Special tools

Apart from the **spark plug spanner no. 60 00 001 258** for the Alpine 1600 S, repairing ALPINE
vehicles of type A 110 requires **only the same special tools** as are used for repairing RENAULT
8 – 10 and 16 vehicles.
