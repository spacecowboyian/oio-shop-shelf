## Replacing the chassis (section C)

For correct replacement, the use of the jig with special ALPINE adapters is **indispensable**.

Order no. of the complete chassis: **60 00 001 667**.

## Replacing the front crossmember (section D)

### Removal

1. Remove: the front axle, the rear axle, the steering, the brake master cylinder.
2. Remove the fuel tank; if it is a steel tank, release the tank's retaining strap.
3. Disconnect the cooling-system lines (with the radiator mounted at the front).
4. Cut out the bulkhead (*Spritzwand*) — the area between the vertical axis of the front crossmember, the
   luggage-compartment floor and the wheel arches.
5. Separate the front crossmember from the central beam, from the pedal-assembly bracket and from the
   longitudinal members with a cutting torch.
6. Separate it from the wheel arches and cut off the two longitudinal members as close as possible to
   the front crossmember.
7. Remove the front crossmember.

### Parts supplied by the spare-parts stores in exchange

- the front crossmember
- 2 front longitudinal members
- 2 connecting plates
- 2 hose holders
- 2 brackets for the spring seats of the bump stops (*Aufschlaggummis*)
- 2 brackets for the steering housing

### Fitting

1. Mount the body on the jig (mounting block for the rear-axle crossmember) and, with the help of the
   wedges, create a distance of **137 ± 1** between the lower part of the central beam and the jig,
   about **100 mm** behind the axis of the front members (this support is necessary because the front
   crossmember is removed).
2. Fit the holder for the anti-roll-bar bearing brackets (05-11-03).
3. Place the front crossmember on the jig fitted with the supports; introduce it into the member.
4. If necessary, fit the dimension **A = A1 = 794 ± 2** (drawing 05-11-03).
5. Place the front crossmember on the jig fitted with the supports; introduce it into the member.
6. If necessary, check the dimension **A = A1 = 794 ± 2** (drawing 05-11-04).
7. Fit the brackets for the spring seats (bore in the axis of the crossmember).
8. Fit the front longitudinal members to the crossmember, at the height of the spring-seat brackets.
9. Fit the connecting plates to the front longitudinal members so that their lower edge coincides with
   the luggage-compartment floor.

### Fitting (continued)

10. Weld on the brake-hose holders.
11. Complete the welds.
12. Using pop rivets, fasten the connecting plates to the wheel arches.
13. Re-join these plates with glass-fibre cloth and synthetic resin.
14. Re-insert the cut-out section into the bulkhead and bond it.
15. Refit the mechanical units; adjust the front axle and the steering-housing height.

## Replacing individual body parts (section F)

In addition to the parts shown on plates 82-01 to 82.09 of PR 871, the following elements are now available as spare parts:

- 1/2 floor panel, right and left
- air-filter partition (*Luftfilter-Trennwand*)
- front luggage-compartment floor and front part of the platform up to the wheel axis
- front air-inlet grille
- sill (door-entry strip), right and left
- front and rear body halves up to the door pillars (inclusive)
- wheel arches, front and rear
- roof, with and without windscreen struts

To cut off body parts we recommend a pneumatic vibrating saw type **SA 70** from the firm Outils
pneumatiques Globe, 143 Av. du Général de Gaulle, 92 - LA-GARENNE-COLOMBES. Replacement saw blades are
supplied under the designation **EP 9239**.
