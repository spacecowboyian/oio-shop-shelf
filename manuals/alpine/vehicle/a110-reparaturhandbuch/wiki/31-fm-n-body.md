## N-1 — Body

### Characteristics

Plastic body (polyester), consisting of two main elements:

- the body floor (*Karosserieboden*);
- the body shell (*Karosserieaufbau*).

These two elements are fixed to a central tubular backbone frame by means of polyester resin and
glass-fibre cloth, and together form a load-bearing unit (approx. **100 kg** for the body and
**50 kg** for the chassis).

Removable body parts:

- doors;
- bonnets (front and rear).

These parts must be individually fitted to each vehicle. As spare parts they are supplied with
maximum dimensions, to allow them to be fitted to the body.

> **NOTE:** on request, ALPINE supplies an even lighter body for racing purposes. The weight
> reduction here can be **30 to 40 kg**.

### Materials used and mixing ratios

1. **Cloth (*Gewebe*):**
   - light glass-fibre cloth of **270 g/m²** (ROVING light);
   - heavy glass-fibre cloth of **520 g/m²** (ROVING heavy);
   - mat (*Filz*) of **300 g/m²**.
2. **Resin:** standard polyester resin, already containing some cure accelerator. Example:
   **STERPON 1069 AC** with cure acceleration of **33 %**. The resin is used to impregnate the
   glass-fibre cloth.
3. **Cure accelerator:** cobalt naphthenate at **6 %**. Example: **NL 51**, added to the hardener
   to speed up its "setting".
4. **Catalyst:** methyl ethyl ketone peroxide. Example: **BUTUNOX** or **TRIGONOX X 40**
   ("faster"). The catalyst causes the "setting" when used.

### Application

When working, **1.5 %** of the weight in catalyst must be added (between **1 and 2 %**, depending
on temperature). At temperatures above **20 °C** reduce the amount of hardener; in cold weather
increase it.

**Polymerisation:** after correct mixing, a chemical curing reaction with heat release occurs in
**15 to 20 min** (depending on the ratio), which makes the resin hard. If the repair is carried
out in an insufficiently heated room (**less than 18 °C**), in a draught, at high humidity or
outdoors, the curing time is extended. It is therefore advisable to warm the surroundings with
infra-red lamps or to increase the proportion of catalyst.

### Repairing the laminated-polyester body

**Repair materials.** The ALPINE spare-parts store supplies, under order no. **6000001354**, a
repair kit with **4 kg** of resin and the associated catalyst, and **2 kg** of light glass-fibre
cloth and mat.

Repairing the laminated-polyester body presents no special problems. The repair methods are
relatively simple and can easily be carried out by non-specialist staff; practically the same
materials and methods are used as in the manufacture of the body.

Broadly, two cases of damage are to be distinguished: either the original body shape is preserved,
or the damaged part no longer bears any resemblance to its original shape.

#### 1/ Repairing minor damage (without replacing parts)

These are mostly small cracks or "frayed" surfaces, i.e. places where the plastic has separated
from the glass-fibre cloth.

## N-2 — Body repair (continued)

First carefully roughen the inner (unpressed) surface to remove all traces of paint or grease and
obtain a surface finish that ensures a good bond. This is often done with a grinding disc; in
places that are hard to reach a wood rasp or a simple scraper also works well. The prepared area
should always extend **5 cm** beyond the edges of the damaged piece.

Then the actual repair begins: the inner cloths are laid first, so that the two edges of a crack
are joined together, or, in a "scratched" zone, the original mechanical strength is restored.

Preferably use light cloths (approx. **250/300 g/m²**), as these are easier to lay. Several layers
can achieve the same thickness as that of the part being repaired.

Always cut the pieces of cloth so that each layer is slightly larger than the one below, to avoid
the edges lying on top of each other and so too abrupt differences in thickness. It is advisable
to impregnate the cloth with resin on a flat metal sheet, then lay it carefully and brush it on.

After the repair and polymerisation of the resin (if necessary with infra-red lamps), finish the
surface: use a file or grinding disc to remove the paint and primer layers at the damaged spot.
Starting from the cloth thus exposed, restore the surface shape by filling with polyester filler.
This is practically the same work as painters do when applying filler to sheet-metal body parts
(see the paragraph "Applying polyurethane paint").

For cracks, chamfer the edges **C = 40 to 50 mm** (see the small figure). Use a grinding disc
(**No. 24**) or a file. Cover the zone exposed in this way with a mixture of resin and cloth, then
apply the polyester filler.

#### 2/ Repair by replacing a part of the body

If the shape of the body was completely lost in the collision, it goes without saying that the
only valid remedy is replacement of the part concerned.

First determine which section of the body must be replaced. The standard replacement parts
supplied by ALPINE are shown on plates **82-05 to 82-08** of the spare-parts catalogue **PR 871**.

1. Cut out the part to be replaced with a saw, then offer up the replacement part.
2. Hold it with small metal plates (approx. **50 x 15 mm**), fixed with two cross-head screws.
3. Then lay the joining cloth as described above.
4. After curing, the plates can be removed for surface finishing: chamfer the edges over a width
   of approx. **5 cm** on both sides of the joint line, lay and impregnate small strips of cloth
   (1 and 2) over the joint, level the surface with polyester filler, prime, sand and paint.

## N-3 — Body repair (concluded) and polyurethane paints

… in no way impairs the value of the vehicle so treated, since the durability of the body is not
reduced. In addition, this work can be carried out by any body shop, provided one or two workers
have attended a course of a few days; this is, however, only really worthwhile when larger repairs
are needed.

The replacement parts are made by the manufacturer; experience shows, however, that this causes no
problems, since the part can be ordered there under the number given in the ALPINE catalogue
**PR 871**. No special, out-of-the-ordinary tools are needed either — only those that workshops
have anyway.

> **NOTE:** brushes and containers are cleaned with acetone.

### Polyurethane paints

a) Applying a polyurethane paint "Vérilac".
b) Painting the inner faces of the bonnets.
c) Painting the dashboard.

#### a) Applying a polyurethane paint "Vérilac"

At present two different paints are used in production: on the one hand nitro (cellulose)
paints, whose application is well known; on the other, polyurethane paints of the **VERILAC**
brand, whose application is described below.

**1/ Identifying the paint.** A rectangular plate in the luggage compartment — on the bulkhead
near the rhomboid plate — gives the paint manufacturer, the type of paint and the colour according
to a code, explained in the appendix (Table 1).

**2/ Products used.** On request, the following products needed for applying polyurethane paints
can be supplied by the ALPINE central spare-parts store:

- **Undercoat (*Unterschicht*):** polyester undercoat **K 89021**; catalyst **TC 50**.
- **Primer (*Grundierung*):** polyester primer **K 89007**; catalyst **817**.
- **Base coat (*Unterlack*):**
  - for metallic Alpine blue no. **331** and yellow no. **3310**: base-coat paint blue
    **AU 49173** or yellow **AU 49124**; clear lacquer **4100 B** (blue 331 only); hardener
    **DU 430**; thinner **D 4047**;
  - for the other colours: paint according to Table 2; hardener **DU 430**; thinner **D 4047**.
- **Top coat (*Lack*):** paint according to Table 2; hardener **DU 430**; thinner **D 4047**.

**3/ Preparing the surfaces.**

- Roughen with abrasive paper **220 – 320 – 400** (wet).
- Repair coarse damage with polyester undercoat **K 89021**, mixed immediately before use as
  follows:
  - undercoat K 89021 = **100 parts**;
  - catalyst TC 50 (yellow) = **2 parts**.

  Only ever mix as much as can be used within **15 to 20 min**. Mix well: the mass must be
  practically white.

  > **Do not increase the dose of catalyst:** any excess prolongs the drying time, which can later
  > lead to the paint lifting.

  Sand.
- Apply the polyester primer. Prepare immediately before use. Pot life: **20 min**.
  - primer K 89007 = **100 parts**;
  - catalyst 817 = **10 parts**.

## N-4 — Polyurethane paints (continued)

Apply **3 to 4 coats** of primer directly one after the other. Drying time: **5 to 6 hours**.
Repair small defects with polyester undercoat. Sand with abrasive paper **400** (wet).

**4/ Applying the paint** (mixing according to Table 2).

- **Applying the base coat:** prepare shortly before use. Usable for **12 hours**. (Special base
  coat for blue 331 and yellow 3310.) Drying at ambient temperature.
- After **15 min**: apply a first coat of paint. Prepare **20 to 30 min** before use; usable for
  **12 hours** (plus clear lacquer for blue 331). Drying at ambient temperature: **15 min**.
- Apply the second coat of paint. Air drying **6 to 8 hours** at ambient temperature; dust-dry
  (can be touched) after **2 to 3 hours**.

> Do not apply the paint to warm or damp surfaces.

**5/ Equipment and quantities required.** For your information, we use **DEVILBISS JGA 502** spray
guns with nozzle **AV 15 EXE**, air cap no. **30**.

Spraying pressure: **4 kg/cm²** for the primer, **3 kg/cm²** for the base coat and the paint.

For a complete respray of the vehicle allow (mixed quantities):

| Product | Quantity (mixed) |
|---|---|
| Primer | 9 kg |
| Base coat | 1.7 kg |
| Paint | 3.3 kg |

#### b) Painting the inner faces of the bonnets

After sanding, spray the multi-colour mix **Gayflex**, which is supplied ready for use.

#### c) Painting the dashboard

To avoid "ring formation" (*Kranzbildung*), it is recommended to treat the dashboard in zones, e.g.
the upper section as far as the cover along the front edge.

1. Graduated roughening of the affected section and of the complete zone.
2. Apply one or two coats — depending on the size of the section to be repaired — of black matt
   crackle paint (*Krakelé-Lack*, nitro), thinned to **50 %** (e.g. **341 0016** from Astral).
3. After **10 min** drying, apply one coat of matt black nitro paint thinned to **50 %** (e.g.
   **341 0014** from Astral).

Drying time: **1 hour** at ambient temperature.
