### Shock absorbers

- Front shock absorbers type R. 1135 — order no. **0855838000**.
- Rear shock absorbers type R. 1135 — order no. **0855916800**.

For motor-sport use, **Koni** or **Bilstein** shock absorbers can also be fitted.
