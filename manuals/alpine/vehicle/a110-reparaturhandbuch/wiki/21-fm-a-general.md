## A-1 — Vehicle design

**Model A. 110.** Rear-wheel-drive vehicle.

**Body:** sports coupé, consisting of a plastic body shell and a central backbone frame
(*Mittelträgerrahmen*).

From the 1970 model year, the Type A.110 vehicles are available in four versions:

| Version | Type |
|---|---|
| Alpine 1300 | 1300 VC |
| Alpine 1300 G | 1300 VA |
| Alpine 1300 S | 1300 VB |
| Alpine 1600 S | 1600 VB — on request with GS engine (racing version, *Rennausführung*) |

Identification is by the rhomboid plate (*Rhombenschild*) fixed in the luggage compartment. The
page shows the plate: "ALPINE — TYPE A110 — N° de Série — Sté des Automobiles ALPINE", with a
Paris address.

Liquid cooling, closed system. Oil cooling by a cooler in a bypass circuit (*Nebenstrom*).

### 4/ Electrical equipment

- Battery 12 Volt – 55 Ah.
- DC dynamo (*Gleichstrom-Lichtmaschine*) for the Alpine 1300, 25 to 30 Amp.
- Alternator (*Drehstromlichtmaschine*) 30/40 Amp for all other versions.

### 5/ Clutch

Single-plate dry clutch. Diaphragm-spring clutch mechanism. Driven plate with a torsionally
elastic hub. Unguided or guided graphite or ball release bearing, depending on version.

### 6/ Gearbox

- Type 330 for the Alpine 1300, or type 353 on request.
- Type 353 for all other versions.
- Type 364 for some versions of the Alpine 1600 S.

Type 330: fully synchronised 4-speed gearbox with one reverse gear.
Types 353/364: fully synchronised 5-speed gearbox with one reverse gear.
Floor-mounted gear lever (*Knüppelschaltung*).

## A-3 — General vehicle data (continued)

### 7/ Transmission

Drive to the rear wheels through half-shafts. One universal joint with needle bearing per shaft.

### 8/ Steering

Rack-and-pinion steering with a compensating spring (*Ausgleichfeder*).

| Item | Standard | On request: more direct steering |
|---|---|---|
| Turns of the steering wheel, lock to lock | 3.2 | 2.5 |
| Reduction ratio | 17 : 1 | 13.5 : 1 |

Steering wheel diameter: 300 mm, 330 mm and 360 mm.

### 9/ Front axle

Independent suspension with wishbones (*Querlenker*).

### 10/ Rear axle

Independent suspension. Mutually independent half-axles, located longitudinally by two
adjustable trailing links (*Zugstreben*).

### 11/ Suspension

Coil springs front and rear. Telescopic shock absorbers at the front (mounted inside the
springs). Four telescopic shock absorbers at the rear, mounted on both sides of the springs.

### 12/ Braking system

Hydraulic operation. Disc brakes on all four wheels. Handbrake acting on the rear wheels through
cables.

### 13/ Tyres and wheels

Michelin X AS – Dunlop SP Sport – V 10 GT.

Recommended tyre pressures in kg/cm²:

| Tyre | Front | Rear |
|---|---|---|
| 15" tyres | 1.4–1.6 | 2.0–2.3 |
| 13" tyres | 1.6–1.8 | 2.3–2.5 |
