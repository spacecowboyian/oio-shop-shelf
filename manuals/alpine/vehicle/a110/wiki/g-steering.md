## Rack and pinion

A rack with compensating spring.

| | Standard (*série*) | Optional ("direct" steering) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pinion | 7-tooth | 9-tooth |
| Multiplication (ratio) | 17/1 | 13.5 |
| Turns of the steering wheel | 3.2 | 2.5 |
| Rack travel per turn of the steering wheel | 44,8 mm | 57.5 mm |

This steering is repairable, and converting the 7-tooth pinion to 9 teeth entails replacing
the **rack rings** — the rack itself is raised by **2 mm**.

## Note — steering rack mounting

Two fixing methods were used.

1. **Welded supports on the crossmember.** The steering mounting holes are slightly
   elongated.
2. **Removable supports bolted to the crossmember.** There are two types of support:
   - standard supports — **H = 55 mm** mounting-hole spacing;
   - taller supports — **H = 56 mm and 57 mm**.

   The dimension is stamped on the **rear face** of the support.

> **IMPORTANT:** After each refit it is imperative to check the variation of toe between
> the low and high positions of the vehicle. Refer to the chapter
> [H — Front Axle](h-front-axle.md).
