## Chapter E: Gearbox (Boîte de vitesses)

**1600 VB:** specification of a new index, **BV 364**.

**BV 364-05:** final drive **11 × 34**, normal; multiplication **3,09**; tachometer
**6 × 10**; conical distance **61.50 mm**. Differs from BV 364-01 and 364-06 only by the
final-drive ratio.

The **BV 353-20** and **364** can be equipped with a **32 × 31** long fifth, multiplication
**0,97**.

The unique conical-distance setting for all indices of **BV 364** is **61.5 mm**.

### Front brakes — installation procedure (Group IV)

#### (a) Chassis support

1. Offer up the aluminium support on the stub-axle carrier, grinding or trimming if
   necessary the passage of the upper and lower part of the pivot.
2. Mount the support with four **8 × 35** square-head screws, reference **07 06 302 900**,
   with nuts and washers.

#### (b) Assembly of the bracket (*chape*)

1. Offer up the bracket and fix it on its support with **12 × 30** screws and washers.
2. For any other details, see **Chapter M of MR 96**.

#### (c) Mounting the hub

Build up the modified **R. 1135** front hub with the centring washer **60 00 001 387** and
the modified **R. 1151** disc, using **10 × 27** screws reference **60 00 001 427**, head
thickness **6.8 cm**, plus nuts and washers.

### Rear brakes — installation procedure (modified stub-axle tube)

#### (a) Support

1. Mount the thrust-tube support, left **60 00 001 435** or right **60 00 001 436**, and the
   bracket support (symmetrical) **60 00 001 437** on the modified stub-axle tube, with
   **8 × 35** square-head screws reference **07 06 302 900**.
2. Build up the bearing cage with its seal, bearing, wheel shaft and studs.
3. Saw off the smooth part of the brake-pad holder stud, reference **06 05 959 400**.
4. Fit a flat washer **3 mm** thick on that stud to compensate for the thickness of the
   thrust-tube support.
5. Mount the cage assembly, bearing and wheel shaft on the stub-axle tube.

#### (b) Mounting hub and disc

1. Mount on the modified **R. 1135** hub the centring washer (moved to the hub) reference
   **60 00 001 387** and the rear disc (thickness **10 mm**) with three **10 × 27** screws,
   head thickness **4.2**, reference **60 00 001 427**.
2. Mount the assembly on the wheel shaft.

#### (c) Assembly of the bracket

1. Offer up the bracket.
2. Grind for clearance if necessary.
3. Fix the bracket with **10 × 35** screws, reference **60 00 001 444**, with washers.

#### (d) Mounting the caliper

Drill a hole of Ø … on the outer front side of the caliper in the piston axis, in order to
be able to re-seat the piston (six-point male key, **7 mm**).
