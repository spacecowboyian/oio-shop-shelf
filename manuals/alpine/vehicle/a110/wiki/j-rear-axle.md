## J-2 — Description and modifications

As for the front axle, changes are made to obtain **negative camber**. These changes are as
follows.

<a id="p46"></a>
### Versions 85 and 1300 G — "S" mounting

Normal, with gearbox **353**:

- Fitting of **12 mm** shims between the crossmember and the gearbox side supports.
- **12 mm** shims between the gearbox and the central gearbox support, which entails
  replacing the studs.

> **NOTE:** **6 mm** shims (*cales*) for versions **1600 S with gearbox 364** and "hard"
> type lateral buffers — Silentblocs No **0996128200**.

<a id="p46"></a>
### Version 1300 G (factory assembly) and 1300 S

- Fitting **23 mm** shims between the crossmember and the gearbox side supports.
- Replacement of the central support (ALPINE support).
- Fitting a tie-rod between the clutch bellhousing and the crossmember.
- "Hard" type lateral buffers — Silentblocs No **0996128200**.

The rear tie-rods (*jambes de force AR*) are also modified: they receive two ends on the
chassis side to replace the Silentblocs. In addition, and as an option, some have a
turnbuckle between the suspension crossmember and the engine block; in that case there is
a need to provide a crossmember with welded threaded ends.

The aim of all these changes is to ensure the rigidity of the rear end, and hence improve
the stability of the vehicle at high speed.

## J-2 — Removal, overhaul and refitting of the rear axle, or replacement of its elements

See **MR 133, Chapter J**.

The stub-axle tubes (*tubes de fusée*) are mounted without any adjustment provision. There
is therefore **no need to act on the eccentric** to obtain toe-in or toe-out at the rear
wheels. On the other hand it is recommended to **check the alignment of the powertrain
relative to the chassis**.
