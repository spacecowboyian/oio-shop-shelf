### Parts A and B

<a id="p45"></a>
Source: [PDF p.45](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0045.png).

**Part A — every 7,500 miles (12,000 km) or 1 year**

- Replace engine oil (printed p.8-6). Capacity without oil filter: 4.5 L (4.8 US qt).
- Check tire inflation and condition every 7,500 miles (12,000 km).

**Part B — every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year**

- Replace engine oil filter (printed p.8-7). Engine oil capacity with filter: 4.8 L (5.1 US qt).
- Check front and rear brakes (printed p.19-3): pads and discs for wear, damage, and cracks; calipers for damage, leaks, and mounting-bolt tightness.
- Check parking brake adjustment (printed p.19-7): 9–13 clicks when the lever is pulled with 196 N (20 kgf, 44 lbf) of force.
- Inspect tie-rod ends, steering gearbox, and boots (printed p.17-5): rack grease, steering linkage, boot damage, and leaking grease.
- Inspect suspension components (printed p.18-3): bolt tightness and ball-joint boot deterioration or damage.
- Inspect driveshaft boots (printed p.16-4): cracks and boot-band tightness.
- Inspect brake hoses and lines, including ABS lines (printed p.19-28), and check the master cylinder, proportioning control valve, and ABS modulator-control unit for damage or leakage.
- Inspect all fluid levels, fluid condition, and leaks: engine coolant (printed p.10-9), manual transmission fluid (p.13-3), brake fluid (p.19-9), clutch fluid (step 1, p.12-6), rear differential fluid (p.15-7), and windshield washer fluid (p.22-184).
- Inspect cooling-system hoses and connections (printed p.10-2) for damage, leaks, and deterioration; check fan operation.
- Inspect exhaust system* (printed p.9-5): catalytic-converter heat shield, exhaust pipe, and muffler for damage, leaks, and tightness.
- Inspect fuel lines* (printed p.11-148) and connections* (p.11-150): loose connections, cracks, and deterioration; retighten loose connections and replace damaged parts.

*The source notes that, under state and federal regulations, failure to perform items marked with an asterisk does not void the customer's emissions warranties. Honda recommends all maintenance at the specified intervals for long-term reliability.*

### Parts C–F

<a id="p46"></a>
Source: [PDF p.46](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0046.png).

**Part C**

- Replace air-cleaner element (printed p.11-169) every 30,000 miles (48,000 km).
- Inspect drive belt (printed p.4-42) every 30,000 miles (48,000 km) or 2 years. Look for cracks and damage and check the auto-tensioner indicator position.
- Replace dust and pollen filter (printed p.21-27) every 30,000 miles (48,000 km) or 2 years. Replace twice as often (every 15,000 miles/24,000 km or 1 year) in areas with high dust, pollen, or soot; replace whenever heating or air-conditioning airflow is below normal.
- Replace rear differential fluid (printed p.15-7) every 30,000 miles (48,000 km) or 2 years. Capacity: 0.74 L (0.78 US qt); use Hypoid Gear Oil GL5 or GL6.

**Part D:** Replace engine coolant (printed p.10-9) at 45,000 miles (72,000 km) or 3 years, then every 30,000 miles (48,000 km) or 2 years. Capacity: 6.5 L (1.72 US gal); use Honda Long Life Antifreeze/Coolant Type 2.

**Part E:** Replace manual transmission fluid (printed p.13-3) every 90,000 miles (144,000 km) or 6 years. Capacity: 1.48 L (1.56 US qt); use Honda MTF.

**Part F**

- Replace spark plugs (printed p.4-28) every 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 7 years: NGK PFR7G-11S or DENSO PK22PR-L11S.
- Inspect valve clearance, cold (printed p.6-10), every 105,000 miles (168,000 km) or 7 years: intake 0.21–0.25 mm (0.008–0.010 in.); exhaust 0.25–0.29 mm (0.010–0.011 in.).

### Parts A–C

<a id="p49"></a>
Source: [PDF p.49](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0049.png).

**Part A — every 3,750 miles (6,000 km) or 6 months**

- Replace engine oil (printed p.8-6) and oil filter (p.8-7). Capacity with oil filter: 4.8 L (5.1 US qt).

**Part B**

- Check tire inflation and condition every 7,500 miles (12,000 km).
- Check front and rear brakes (printed p.19-3) every 7,500 miles (12,000 km) or 1 year: pads and discs for wear, damage, and cracks; calipers for damage, leaks, and mounting-bolt tightness.
- Inspect tie-rod ends, steering gearbox, and boots (printed p.17-5) every 7,500 miles (12,000 km) or 1 year: rack grease, steering linkage, boot damage, and leaking grease.
- Inspect suspension components (printed p.18-3) every 7,500 miles (12,000 km) or 1 year: bolt tightness and ball-joint boot deterioration or damage.
- Inspect driveshaft boots (printed p.16-4) every 7,500 miles (12,000 km) or 1 year: cracks and boot-band tightness.

**Part C**

- Inspect brake hoses and lines, including ABS lines (printed p.19-28), every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year. Check the master cylinder, proportioning control valve, and ABS modulator-control unit for damage or leakage.
- Check parking brake adjustment (printed p.19-7) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year: 9–13 clicks when the lever is pulled with 196 N (20 kgf, 44 lbf) of force.
- Replace air-cleaner element (printed p.11-169) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year.
- Replace rear differential fluid (printed p.15-7) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year. Capacity: 0.74 L (0.78 US qt); use Hypoid Gear Oil GL5 or GL6.
- Lubricate all hinges, locks, and latches with multipurpose grease.
- Check all lights (printed p.22-110): interior and exterior light function and headlight position.
- Inspect all fluid levels, fluid condition, and leaks every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year: engine coolant (printed p.10-9), manual transmission fluid (p.13-3), brake fluid (p.19-9), clutch fluid (step 1, p.12-6), rear differential fluid (p.15-7), and windshield washer fluid (p.22-184).
- Inspect cooling-system hoses and connections (printed p.10-2) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year for damage, leaks, and deterioration; check fan operation.
- Inspect exhaust system* (printed p.9-5) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year: catalytic-converter heat shield, exhaust pipe, and muffler for damage, leaks, and tightness.
- Inspect fuel lines* (printed p.11-148) and connections* (p.11-150) every 15,000 miles (24,000 km) or 1 year: loose connections, cracks, and deterioration; retighten loose connections and replace damaged parts.
- Inspect vehicle underbody: check paint for damage, scratches, stone chipping, and dents.

*The source notes that, under state and federal regulations, failure to perform items marked with an asterisk does not void the customer's emissions warranties. Honda recommends all maintenance at the specified intervals for long-term reliability.*

### Resetting the Maintenance Information Display

Stop the vehicle before resetting. The source warns that failing to reset after service, or resetting without doing the service, can make future maintenance timing inaccurate and cause serious mechanical problems. Engine-oil life and individual maintenance items can be reset independently only with the HDS.

1. Turn the ignition switch ON (II).
2. Press and release the trip button repeatedly until the engine-oil-life indicator appears. *(The source prints “Press release”.)*
3. Hold the trip button for about 10 seconds. The oil-life indicator and maintenance codes blink. If oil life is above 15%, complete any required maintenance before resetting.
4. Hold the trip button for another 5 seconds. The maintenance codes disappear and oil life resets to 100%.

<a id="p70"></a>
Source: [PDF p.70](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0070.png).

For engine electrical maintenance, the source includes this SRS safety notice. The S2000 has a driver airbag in the steering-wheel hub, a passenger airbag above the glove box, and seat-belt tensioners in the retractors. Items marked with an asterisk on the contents page include or lie near SRS components. Servicing, disassembling, or replacing them requires special precautions and tools and should be done by an authorized Honda dealer.

- To avoid making the SRS inoperative, with risk of injury or death in a severe frontal collision, have all SRS service work done by an authorized Honda dealer.
- Incorrect SRS removal or installation can unintentionally activate airbags or seat-belt tensioners and cause injury.
- Do not bump or impact the SRS unit or front impact sensors while ignition is ON (II), or for at least 3 minutes after turning ignition OFF. The system may otherwise fail in a collision or airbags may deploy.
- SRS connectors are yellow. Related components are in the steering column, console, dashboard, lower dashboard panel, and above the glove box. Do not use electrical test equipment on these circuits.
