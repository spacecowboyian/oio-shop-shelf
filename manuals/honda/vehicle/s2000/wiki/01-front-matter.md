## How to Use This Manual — PDF p.1

This manual is divided into multiple sections. The first page of each section is marked with a black tab that lines up with its corresponding thumb index tab on the introduction page and the back cover. The symbols printed at the top corner of each page can also be used as a quick reference.

Each section includes:

1. A table of contents or an exploded-view index showing:
   - Parts disassembly sequence.
   - Bolt torques and thread sizes.
   - Page references to descriptions in text.
2. Disassembly/assembly procedures and tools.
3. Inspection.
4. Testing/troubleshooting.
5. Repair.
6. Adjustments.

The introduction page's thumb index lists General Information, Specifications, Maintenance, Engine Electrical, Engine Mechanical, Engine Cooling, Fuel and Emissions, Transaxle, Steering, Suspension, Brakes (Including VSA), Body, Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning, Body Electrical, and Restraints. An asterisk marks sections that include SRS components; special precautions are required when servicing them.

## Safety Messages — PDF p.1

The manual provides safety messages and other safety information, but cannot warn about every hazard associated with servicing the vehicle. It directs the reader to use their own good judgment.

Safety information appears as:

- **Safety Labels** on the vehicle.
- **Safety Messages** preceded by a safety alert symbol and one of three signal words:
  - **DANGER:** You WILL be KILLED or SERIOUSLY HURT if you don't follow instructions.
  - **WARNING:** You CAN be KILLED or SERIOUSLY HURT if you don't follow instructions.
  - **CAUTION:** You CAN be HURT if you don't follow instructions.
- **Instructions** describing how to service the vehicle correctly and safely.

The OCR damaged the signal-word graphics; the words and definitions above were checked against the [page image](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0001.png).

## Supplemental Restraint System (SRS) — PDF p.2

The S2000 SRS includes a driver's airbag in the steering wheel hub, a passenger's airbag in the dashboard above the glove box, and seat belt tensioners in the seat belt retractors. The manual says that items marked with an asterisk on the contents page include or are located near SRS components. Servicing, disassembling, or replacing these items requires special precautions and tools and should be done by an authorized Honda dealer.

The manual gives these SRS safety instructions:

- To avoid rendering the SRS inoperative, which could lead to personal injury or death in a severe frontal collision, all SRS service work should be done by an authorized Honda dealer.
- Improper service procedures, including incorrect removal and installation of the SRS, could lead to personal injury from unintentional activation of the airbags and seat belt tensioners.
- Do not bump or impact the SRS unit or front impact sensors when the ignition switch is **ON (II)**, or for at least **3 minutes** after the ignition switch is turned **OFF**; otherwise, the system may fail in a collision or airbags may deploy.
- SRS electrical connectors are identified by yellow color coding. Related components are in the steering column, console, dashboard, dashboard lower panel, and dashboard above the glove box. Do not use electrical test equipment on these circuits.

The **3-minute** duration and other SRS wording were checked against [PDF p.2](https://github.com/spacecowboyian/oio-shop-shelf/blob/manuals-s2000/manuals/honda/vehicle/s2000/page-images/p0002.png).
