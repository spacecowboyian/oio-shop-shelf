# Rear tray trim, except CR — diagram continuation

The roll bar and upper trim assembly cannot be separated. First remove the rear console and rear side trim. Detach the harness clip and disconnect the speaker connectors; on 2006–2008 models, also detach the harness clip, release the harness grommet, and disconnect the rear-window defogger connector on the left side. Slip the seat belt through the roll-bar upper-trim opening. Remove the seat before removing the lower seat-belt anchor bolt.

- Fasteners: C, eight bolts; D, four screws; E, F and G, one clip each.
- Upper and lower seat-belt anchor bolts: 7/16-20 UNF, **32 N·m (3.3 kgf·m, 24 lbf·ft)**.
- Illustrated roll-bar bolts: 8 × 1.25 mm, **22 N·m (2.2 kgf·m, 16 lbf·ft)**.
- Convertible-top cover pins: outside and middle for 2000–2001; outside only for 2002–2008. A 6 × 1.0 mm fastener is shown at the pin.

See the linked diagram for the numbered removal sequence, clip positions, and the 2000–2001 versus 2002–2008 trim shapes.

# Supplemental Restraint System (SRS)

## General Information
The S2000 SRS includes a driver's airbag in the steering wheel hub, a passenger's airbag in the dashboard above the glove box, and seat belt tensioners in the seat belt retractors. Information necessary to safely service the SRS is included in this Service Manual.

Items marked with an asterisk (*) on the contents page include or are located near SRS components. Servicing, disassembling, or replacing these items requires special precautions and tools, and should be done by an authorized Honda dealer.

## Safety Warnings

1.  **To avoid rendering the SRS inoperative**, which could lead to personal injury or death in the event of a severe frontal collision, all SRS service work should be done by an authorized Honda dealer.
2.  **Improper service procedures**, including incorrect removal and installation of the SRS, could lead to personal injury caused by unintentional activation of the airbags and seat belt tensioners.
3.  **Do not bump or impact the SRS unit, or front impact sensors** when the ignition switch is ON (II), or for at least 3 minutes after the ignition switch is turned OFF; otherwise, the system may fail in a collision, or airbags may deploy.
4.  **SRS electrical connectors are identified by yellow color coding.** Related components are located in the steering column, console, dashboard, dashboard lower panel, in the dashboard above the glove box. Do not use electrical test equipment on these circuits.
