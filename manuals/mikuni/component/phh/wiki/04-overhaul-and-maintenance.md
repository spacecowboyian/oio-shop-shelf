    Most carburetor malfunctions can be ascribed, with the exception of mechanical breakdowns,
to improper air-fuel mixture ratio, which is generally caused by clogged jets, air passages or fuel
passages, and to a change in the fuel level. It is therefore important to keep the components clean at
all times so that air and fuel will flow properly.
